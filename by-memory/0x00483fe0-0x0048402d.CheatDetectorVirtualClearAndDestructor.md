## Behavior

`0x00483fe0` is the `CheatDetector` override for the `TimerHandler` callback-shaped virtual slot. `0x00483ff0` is `ClearCheatDetectorSingleton`, a no-argument cleanup helper that clears singleton storage `0x0067ab3c` and returns. Its only known reachability is constructor cleanup/unwind metadata at `0x005fc446`, matching the constructor's early publication of `this` into `g_pCheatDetector`. This helper is lifecycle support for the class, not an ordinary public `CheatDetector` instance method and not an application cleanup routine.

`0x00484000` is the `CheatDetector` scalar deleting destructor wrapper.
