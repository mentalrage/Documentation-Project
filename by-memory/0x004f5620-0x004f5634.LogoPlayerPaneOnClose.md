## Behavior

The close handler marks the pane for deferred deletion through the shared [UID:0001EA][0x00544460-0x00545086.PaneCore](by-memory/0x00544460-0x00545086.PaneCore.md) helper at `0x00544690`, then invokes the completion callback stored at `this + 0x10c` through callback virtual slot `+0x0c`.
