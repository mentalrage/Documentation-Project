## IDA MCP Evidence

## Ownership Decision

This is a coherent root/background pane island. The `BackPane` and `BackGroundPane` classes should be documented and migrated together initially as `map/BackPane.cpp`, while the later `BalloonObjectPane` island starts a separate map-object overlay module.

Do not fold [UID:0000L3][MapPane](by-file/MapPane.md) into this file. `MapPane` owns game-map tiles, objects, packets, and render passes; `BackPane` owns the root backdrop and shared bounds/container behavior.
