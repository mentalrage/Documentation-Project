## Ownership Decision

Treat `0x0041b110` as the shared download dispatcher. Keep the dispatcher in [UID:0000JC][FileDownloader](by-file/FileDownloader.md), while cross-linking feature semantics to [UID:0000LE][MiniMap](by-file/MiniMap.md), [UID:0000I1][CashShopVersionRequest](by-file/CashShopVersionRequest.md), and [UID:0000JE][FittingRoom](by-file/FittingRoom.md).

The downloader request submission helpers at `0x0041b180`, `0x0041b200`, and `0x0041b270` remain a boundary case. They are adjacent to the small cash-shop payload class, but live caller evidence passes the FileDownloader-lifetime singleton [UID:0000QH][g_pCashShopRequest](by-global/g_pCashShopRequest.md) / `dword_67A738`. Keep them cross-linked instead of forcing a final owner from adjacency alone.
