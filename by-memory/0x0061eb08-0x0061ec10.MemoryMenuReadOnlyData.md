## Summary

This `.rdata` span contains the `MemoryMan`, `MenuPane`, `MenuItem`, `StringMenuItem`, and `SeparatorMenuItem` vtables plus the menu background resource name. It ends at the `MerchantDialogPane` vtable.

Parent handling: keep `AUTOGEN_PARENT_UID` blank.
