## Storage Role

This 4-byte data slot stores [UID:0000RT][g_pNewHumanImageLib](by-global/g_pNewHumanImageLib.md), the process-wide pointer to the new human sprite composition library. The constructor writes the active instance, destructor/helper paths clear it, and `Application::Shutdown` reads it during image-library teardown.
