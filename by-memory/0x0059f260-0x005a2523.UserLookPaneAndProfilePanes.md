## Ownership And State Notes

- [UID:0000P0][UserLookPane](by-file/UserLookPane.md) owns the remote-character look/profile source module. [UID:0000JQ][GeneralPurposePanel](by-file/GeneralPurposePanel.md) constructs the panel but should not absorb the parser/render/view-switch implementation.
- [UID:00008Q][MoreInfoPane](by-class/MoreInfoPane.md) and [UID:0000AV][ProfilePane](by-class/ProfilePane.md) are local read-only child panes. They are allocated by `UserLookPane::UserLookPane`, receive local vtable families, and expose only two always-false virtual gates each.
- [UID:00026V][0x0062eccc-0x0062ef0c.UserLookReadOnlyData](by-memory/0x0062eccc-0x0062ef0c.UserLookReadOnlyData.md) is the `.rdata` counterpart for this executable range. It records the `UserLookPane`, `MoreInfoPane`, and `ProfilePane` vtables plus user-look/profile resource strings.
- [UID:0002A8][0x0069bed4-0x0069bed8.MoreInfoPaneSingletonSlot](by-memory/0x0069bed4-0x0069bed8.MoreInfoPaneSingletonSlot.md) records the constructor write of the `MoreInfoPane` child pointer into `dword_69BED4`; no direct reads are currently known, so source-level retention of that global remains open.
- The parser owns profile/look payload ingestion and uses the shared image decode path through [UID:000175][0x004d05f0-0x004d0f4a.ImageDecodeWrappers](by-memory/0x004d05f0-0x004d0f4a.ImageDecodeWrappers.md). It should stay in the user-look panel source, not in generic profile-storage or image-resource modules.

## Evidence Notes

- IDA lists the same function starts and ends in this range, with vtable xrefs for the virtual methods and constructor xref `0x004b8477`.
- IDA decompilation of the constructor installs `MoreInfoPane::vftable` and `ProfilePane::vftable` on two allocated child panes, then calls `LegendPane::LegendPane` at `0x0056c400`.
- IDA decompilation of `0x0059f500` is the destructor cleanup path without the scalar-delete flag branch. It releases child panes, the object at `this + 0xd9c`, the `AUTOBUF<unsigned char>` storage at `this + 0xb8c`, and the base `PanelPane`.
- Wave3 models the scalar deleting destructor at `0x005a23f0` and the excluded adjustor thunks at `0x005a23d5`/`0x005a23e0`, but does not model the real `0x0059f500` helper.
- 2026-05-26 IDA MCP `list_globals` confirms `UserLookPane` vtable bases at `0x0062eccc`, `0x0062ed1c`, and `0x0062ed4c`; `MoreInfoPane` at `0x0062ed58`, `0x0062edd0`, and `0x0062ee00`; and `ProfilePane` at `0x0062ee0c`, `0x0062ee84`, and `0x0062eeb4`. Current generated metadata reports no vtables for these classes. See [UID:0001Y0][LookPaneVtableFamily](by-type/by-vtable/LookPaneVtableFamily.md).
