## Detailed Behavior

This span is the middle SoundManager playback-control layer between track selection and local music scanning. It does not choose DAT playlist files itself; [UID:0001I7][0x005797b0-0x0057a25c.SoundManagerMusicSelection](by-memory/0x005797b0-0x0057a25c.SoundManagerMusicSelection.md) resolves music paths, then these helpers stop, pause, resume, advance, or adjust the active stream/sample state.

The stream-control helpers stop the active stream, expose and clamp the stream/master volume, apply volume changes to active Miles stream/Redbook state, mute and unmute music, pause/resume the stream, and advance either loose/local or DAT playlist playback. They are the control surface used by UI and audio restart paths, while Redbook-specific track order and repeat behavior remains documented in [UID:0001IB][0x0057b020-0x0057b48a.SoundManagerRedbookPlayback](by-memory/0x0057b020-0x0057b48a.SoundManagerRedbookPlayback.md).

The sample/SFX helpers play cached sound-effect payloads, expose and clamp sample volume, apply volume to active sample handles, and toggle the sound-effect enabled flag. These pair with constructor evidence in [UID:0000NV][SoundManager](by-file/SoundManager.md), which records the DAT-backed `%03d.wav` preload loop for sound-effect resources.

`GetDirectSound` is the narrow DirectSound bridge used by video/audio integration. [UID:0001Q8][client_audio](by-meta/client_audio.md) keeps Bink playback outside SoundManager while noting that the video setup asks SoundManager for DirectSound.

`OnTimerEvent` handles SoundManager timer callbacks, including Redbook track-ended handling, stream-stop events, and stream fade steps. The timer scheduling/cancel wrappers at `0x00597610` and `0x00597630` are deliberately not part of this SoundManager page: [UID:0001K9][0x00597610-0x00597645.TimerHandlerWrappers](by-memory/0x00597610-0x00597645.TimerHandlerWrappers.md) proves they are generic `TimerHandler`/`TimerMgr` wrappers forwarded through [UID:0000SI][g_pTimerMgr](by-global/g_pTimerMgr.md).
