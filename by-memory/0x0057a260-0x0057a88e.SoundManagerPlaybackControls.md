## Ownership Decision

These methods are core `SoundManager` playback controls. They should stay in `audio/SoundManager.cpp`. The tiny timer-manager wrappers at `0x00597610` and `0x00597630` are generic [UID:0001K9][0x00597610-0x00597645.TimerHandlerWrappers](by-memory/0x00597610-0x00597645.TimerHandlerWrappers.md), even though current generated SoundManager source still calls through them.

Keep final C++ blank despite the score gate because original method spellings, raw chunk representation, exact class member names, and final timer-event enum names are still not recoverable safely from current evidence.
