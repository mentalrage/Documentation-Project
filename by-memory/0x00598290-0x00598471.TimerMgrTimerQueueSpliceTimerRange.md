## Behavior

Removes a contiguous logical range from the timer queue. It compares the number of entries before and after the removed span, shifts whichever side is cheaper, copies 20-byte scheduled-event records to close the hole, decrements count, adjusts the logical start index when shifting from the head side, and returns an iterator-like result pointing to the post-splice position.

The stable source-level behavior is clearer than IDA's pointer-chain decompiler names:

- Uses `this[3]` as `startIndex` and `this[4]` as `count`.
- Computes entries before the erased range as `eraseBegin - startIndex` and entries after the erased range as `(startIndex + count) - eraseEnd`.
- If the head side is cheaper, copies preceding records backward toward `eraseEnd`, decrements `count` once per erased entry, and increments `startIndex` unless the queue becomes empty.
- If the tail side is cheaper, copies following records forward toward `eraseBegin`, decrements `count` once per erased entry, and resets `startIndex` if the queue becomes empty.
- Copies each [UID:0001VX][ScheduledTimerEvent](by-type/by-struct/ScheduledTimerEvent.md) as a 16-byte block plus the trailing dword at offset `0x10`, proving the `0x14` record stride.
- Returns an iterator-like record through the caller-provided output pointer: it clears the first two dwords, writes the post-splice logical position at output `+0x08`, and copies a root/sentinel-derived pointer when available.
