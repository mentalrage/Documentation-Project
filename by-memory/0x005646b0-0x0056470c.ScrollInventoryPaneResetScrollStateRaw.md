## Behavior

This raw helper clears scrollbar interaction state after capture or hover. It calls the shared capture/interaction cleanup helper at `0x00597600` on `this + 0xa4`, sets byte `this + 0x104` to `0xff`, and if the previous highlighted part at `this + 0x103` was not `0xff`, it computes that part rectangle through `0x005640a0` and invalidates it through the pane virtual slot at `+0x20`. It then clears `this + 0x103` to `0xff`.
