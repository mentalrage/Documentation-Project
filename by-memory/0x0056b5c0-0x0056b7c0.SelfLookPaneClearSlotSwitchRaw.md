## Behavior

The helper is a raw `thiscall`-style slot-clear/update helper. `ecx` is the `SelfLookPane` instance, `[ebp+8]` is the packet pointer, and the only packet field consumed by this body is the byte at `packet+1`. The byte is normalized by subtracting one, values above `0x16` use the default path, and recognized entries jump through the local byte map and jump table.

Every path invalidates the pane rectangle at `this+0x44` through the virtual at vtable slot `+0x20` and returns `0`.
