## Behavior

Copies a 16-byte caller-provided rectangle into the shared `MenuItem` bounds rectangle at `this + 0x04`.

This is the setter half of a retained paired API with [UID:0002JJ][0x00516fb0-0x00516fc1.MenuItemGetBounds](by-memory/0x00516fb0-0x00516fc1.MenuItemGetBounds.md). Both methods copy the complete 16-byte rectangle with `movups`, are fenced as independent bodies, and are absent from the `MenuItem` vtable, which makes them ordinary nonvirtual member methods rather than virtual slots or compiler data.
