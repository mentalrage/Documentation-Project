## Behavior

This raw helper resets the volume slider's interaction state. It stops the timer/update view through `this + 0xa4`, reads the previous highlighted part at `this + 0x103`, clears the active interaction part at `this + 0x104` to `0xff`, and if the old highlighted part was not `0xff`, computes that part rectangle through `0x00565010` and invalidates it through the pane virtual slot at `+0x20`. It then clears `+0x103` to `0xff`.
