## Behavior

1. Stops the timer/update-view path through the tertiary/timer-handler view at `this+0xa4` by calling `0x00597600`.
2. Reads the old highlighted part byte from `this+0x103`.
3. Clears the active interaction part at `this+0x104` to the no-part sentinel `0xff`.
4. If the old highlighted part was not `0xff`, computes its rectangle through `0x00565010` and invalidates it through the pane virtual slot at `+0x20`.
5. Clears the highlighted part byte at `this+0x103` to `0xff`.
6. Exits through the raw helper's security-cookie epilogue and plain `retn` immediately before the `0x005654ec` destructor adjustor thunk.
