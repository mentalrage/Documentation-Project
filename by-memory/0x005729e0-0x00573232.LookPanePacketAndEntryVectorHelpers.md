## Packet Helper Evidence

The three raw packet helpers are small unmodeled send wrappers. Live IDA confirms that `0x005729e0-0x00572a54`, `0x00572a60-0x00572ad4`, and `0x00572ae0-0x00572b54` have no IDA function object, no direct start xrefs, a repeated four-call byte-writing pattern through `0x00575380`, and a final dispatch through `0x00574bb0` using the packet manager global at `dword_67A7EC`. The observed payload shape is four bytes: opcode `0x1b`, a constant `1`, the per-helper subtype (`0x02`, `0x08`, or `0x0b`), and a trailing zero byte.

## Look Update Evidence

The raw `0x00572b60-0x00572f25` body is not an IDA function, but live disassembly identifies it as a larger `SelfLookPane2` update/parser path rather than padding or a compiler artifact. It reads packet/string data through helpers at `0x00575480`, `0x00575470`, `0x005754c0`, `0x004d1fa0`, and `0x004d2640`, references `dword_67A748`, stores pane state from offsets near `this+0x108` through `this+0x15d`, and conditionally invalidates the pane with `call dword ptr [edx+20h]` after pushing `this+0x44`.

Observed field groups are stable enough for reconstruction notes but not final naming: the switch writes the `+0x14c/+0x14e`, `+0x150/+0x152`, `+0x154/+0x156`, `+0x158/+0x15c`, and `+0x15a/+0x15d` pairs for update cases, and the tail compares values against `this+0x960` and `this+0x964` before deciding whether to redraw. This keeps the helper with `SelfLookPane2`, but the final field names remain below final-source quality.
