## Boundaries

The range starts immediately after the older `OptionPane` helper island and stops before alignment padding leading to `FriendListDialog` at `0x0053f2c0`. The scalar deleting destructor wrappers for these classes live later in the shared destructor island around `0x005427a0`, `0x00542800`, and `0x00542a00`.

IDA symbol enumeration confirms the matching decorated vtable families at `0x00620d10-0x00620ee0`; see [UID:0001Y1][MacroDialogFamilyVtables](by-type/by-vtable/MacroDialogFamilyVtables.md). Those vtable addresses are real even though current generated metadata reports `vtable_count: 0` for these classes.
