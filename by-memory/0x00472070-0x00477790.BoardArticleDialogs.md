- Current `class_BulletinDialog.meta_wave3` reports `vtable_count: 0` even though IDA confirms three `BulletinDialog` table bases. See [UID:0001X5][BulletinDialogVtables](by-type/by-vtable/BulletinDialogVtables.md).
- Current board/article dialog metadata also reports `vtable_count: 0` for the neighboring dialog, pane, and alert classes even though IDA confirms the [UID:0001X4][BoardArticleDialogVtableFamily](by-type/by-vtable/BoardArticleDialogVtableFamily.md).
- Several `ArticleListPane` helpers at `0x004f3c00+` look generic enough that their final owner may be a shared list-pane helper, not the board/article dialog file.

## Ownership Decision
