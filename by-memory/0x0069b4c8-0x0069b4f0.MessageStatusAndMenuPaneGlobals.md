## IDA MCP Evidence

- `py_eval` xref scan reports all ten dword slots named and referenced in IDA, with `.data` bytes initialized to `0xffffffff`.
- Decompilation confirms direct singleton writes for `OldSystemMessagePane`, `ParcelIconPane`, `SoundStatusPane`, `TimerPane`, `UrlAlertPane`, `UserListDialogPane`, `UserStatusPane2`, `MenuVarietyPane`, and `OldUserStatusPane`.
