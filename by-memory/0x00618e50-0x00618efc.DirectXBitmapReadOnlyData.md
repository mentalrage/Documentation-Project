## Summary

This small `.rdata` span contains `DIBitmap` and `DirectX` RTTI/vtables plus a DirectX installation/error string. It starts at the `DIBitmap` RTTI pointer and ends before the `EmployeeDialogPane` RTTI pointer at `0x00618efc`.
