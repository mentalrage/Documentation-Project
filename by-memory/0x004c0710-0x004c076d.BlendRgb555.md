## Behavior

The routine blends two RGB555 pixels without touching global state or calling other functions. It keeps the green channel separate with mask `0x03e0`, keeps red/blue together with mask `0x7c1f`, multiplies the first color by the supplied weight, multiplies the second color by `32 - weight`, shifts both accumulated channel groups right by five, masks them back to RGB555 lanes, and ORs the result together.

This is handwritten/source-owned render math rather than CRT/compiler glue. Final source should keep it as a small free helper under the `SoftwareBlend16` render grouping; the name/signature remain provisional, so no final reconstruction C++ is emitted here.
