## Notes

Generated source currently labels part of the destructor path through `TextButtonExControlPane`, but constructor evidence and broad caller xrefs identify this as a `Pane`-derived panel base.

IDA MCP caller checks show direct constructor calls from `InventoryPane`, `NewInventoryPane`, `NewOptionPane`, `SelfLookPane`, `GroupPane`, `GroupPane2`, `NewGroupPane`, `CollectionPane`, `SelfLookPane2`, `SpellInventoryPane`, `NewSpellInventoryPane`, `NewSystemMessagePane`, and `UserLookPane`.

IDA MCP `xrefs_to` shows `0x00545110` referenced by both adjustor thunks plus vtable data at `0x00621a70`; the two adjustor thunks have data xrefs at `0x00621ac0` and `0x00621af0`.

IDA `disasm` shows the non-deleting destructor at `0x005450d0` reinstalls all three `PanelPane` vtable bases, then tail-jumps to the `Pane` teardown at `0x00544580`. This directly contradicts the active generated source label that routes through `TextButtonExControlPane`.

The `PanelPane` vtable family uses primary, secondary, and tertiary bases at `0x00621a70`, `0x00621ac0`, and `0x00621af0`. The tertiary table has only two `PanelPane` slots; the dword at `0x00621af8` begins neighboring `EditablePaperPane` RTTI metadata and must not be counted as a `PanelPane` slot.
