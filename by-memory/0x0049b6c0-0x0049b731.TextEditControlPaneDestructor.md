## Evidence Notes

- `lookup_funcs(0x0049b6c0)` reports size `0x71`, giving exclusive range `0x0049b6c0-0x0049b731`.
- `xrefs_to(0x0049b6c0)` reports four references: the two adjustor thunks and primary vtable cells at `0x00618100` and `0x006181a4`.
- The primary vtable cell at `0x00618100` belongs to the `TextEditControlPane` vtable set. The `0x006181a4` cell is associated with the neighboring StaticTextControlPane family, so this page documents the shared-reference caveat instead of assigning caller ownership to StaticTextControlPane.
- Decompilation and disassembly both show three `TextEditControlPane` vtable stores at object offsets `+0x000`, `+0x0a0`, and `+0x0a4`.
- The body reads the embedded editor/control pointer at offset `+0x10c`, calls that object's virtual deleting destructor with flag `1` when present, then clears the pointer slot.
- `callees(0x0049b6c0)` reports the base pane teardown helper at `0x00544580`, guard-check helper at `0x0041b6a0`, and object-free helper at `0x004f4ac0`.
- The deleting flag path frees the wrapper only when bit `0x1` is set and bit `0x4` is clear; otherwise it returns the wrapper without freeing it.
- Boundary padding from live bytes: `0x0049b6bc-0x0049b6c0` is four `0xcc` bytes and `0x0049b731-0x0049b740` is fifteen `0xcc` bytes.
