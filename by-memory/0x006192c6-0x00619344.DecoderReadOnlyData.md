## Summary

This `.rdata` span contains two zero terminator/boundary bytes after the preceding AddEmployee alert string, the `Encoder` RTTI/vtable pair, the `Decoder` RTTI/vtable pair, six adjacent DAT-parser constant vectors, and the `Error` RTTI pointer immediately before `??_7Error@@6B@`.
