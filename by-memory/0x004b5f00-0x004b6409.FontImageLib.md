## Ownership Summary

This page groups the `FontImageLib` executable method island from constructor through scalar deleting destructor. The class is the singleton font-glyph image library behind [UID:0000QX][g_pFontImageLib](by-global/g_pFontImageLib.md), uses [UID:0001XM][FontImageLibVtable](by-type/by-vtable/FontImageLibVtable.md), lazily opens the two `BARAM%02d.EFT` font archives, builds two 65536-entry glyph-pointer tables, and decodes compact glyph rows into caller-provided [UID:00004I][EPFTileContext](by-class/EPFTileContext.md) state.

Do not move GrafPort text iteration, suffix fitting, line-height, or draw-loop ownership into this aggregate. Those higher-level consumers call the FontImageLib metrics/measure/decode services through the singleton and are tracked on [UID:00016C][0x004ba9a0-0x004bad66.GrafPortTextRunHelpers](by-memory/0x004ba9a0-0x004bad66.GrafPortTextRunHelpers.md), [UID:00016I][0x004bb5e0-0x004bb7df.GrafPortDrawGlyph](by-memory/0x004bb5e0-0x004bb7df.GrafPortDrawGlyph.md), and related text-layout pages.
