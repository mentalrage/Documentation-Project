## Evidence Notes

- The `TextEditObject`, `TextEditScrap`, and `EPFTextEditObject` ranges are tightly coupled to text-edit storage, clipboard, and inline symbol payloads.
- `TextFilter` is adjacent but has application-startup singleton evidence and broader chat/mail/input sanitizer use.
- IDA MCP reports direct constructor callers for `EPFTextEditObject` at `0x00566da3`, `0x005707c1`, and `0x005a0277`.
- IDA MCP reports one `TextFilter` constructor caller at `0x004f615f`.
- 2026-05-26 IDA MCP recheck confirms the separate [UID:0001JU][0x00595760-0x005958fe.TextFilterAndSanitizer](by-memory/0x00595760-0x005958fe.TextFilterAndSanitizer.md) page: `g_pTextFilter` is at `0x0067adc8`, the one-slot `TextFilter` vtable is at `0x0062e188`, and `SanitizeWideTextForChat` has broad user-text submission callers.
- 2026-05-28 IDA MCP recheck confirms the `TextFilter` scalar deleting destructor at `0x005958d0` ends at `0x005958fe`; `0x005958fe-0x00595900` is `0xcc` alignment.
