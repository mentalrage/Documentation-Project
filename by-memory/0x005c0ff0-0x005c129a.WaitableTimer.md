- The constructor stores the `WaitableTimer` vtable, calls `g_pfnCreateEventW` / `dword_69BE3C` with four zero arguments, stores the completion event at `+0x04`, throws a `Win32Error`-style exception if creation fails, initializes the critical section at `+0x08`, clears `m_isRunning` at `+0x20`, and clears `m_timerId` / `m_callbackGateEvent` at `+0x24/+0x28`.

- The raw start helper sets `m_isRunning`, creates a second event through `g_pfnCreateEventW`, stores it as `m_callbackGateEvent`, loops on `timeSetEvent(delayMilliseconds, 5, 0x005c11c0, this, 0)` until the returned timer id is nonzero, stores `m_timerId`, and signals the callback gate after registration succeeds.
- The raw cancel helper enters the critical section and, without a branch or null guard, clears `m_isRunning`, calls `timeKillEvent(m_timerId)`, closes `m_callbackGateEvent`, resets `m_completionEvent`, clears `m_timerId` and `m_callbackGateEvent`, and leaves the critical section. This no-guard behavior is preserved on [UID:0002L9][0x005c1170-0x005c11b5.WaitableTimerCancelMultimediaTimer](by-memory/0x005c1170-0x005c11b5.WaitableTimerCancelMultimediaTimer.md).
