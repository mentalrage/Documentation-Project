## Source / ABI Disposition

The highest-probability source shape is an explicitly declared public virtual destructor with an out-of-line empty definition. The linker retained this standalone complete-object body even though it has no inbound code/data route and the reachable scalar deleting wrapper independently duplicates all teardown. It also appears immediately after the constructor and before the next virtual method in the same ordinary class-method run. The same constructor/destructor/virtual-handler ordering is repeated for the neighboring `SpellMacroDialog`, `NewMacroDialog`, and `IntegrateMacroDialog` families.

Virtual status is not optional source behavior: the primary vtable destructor slot points to UID00038C, the two adjusted views route through deleting-wrapper thunks, and inherited `DialogPane::~DialogPane()` is virtual.
