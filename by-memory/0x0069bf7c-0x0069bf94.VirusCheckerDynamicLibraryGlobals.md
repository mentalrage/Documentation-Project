## Reconstruction Notes

These are source-declared static globals or static class/module variables owned by `VirusChecker`. The rebuilt project should declare typed `HMODULE` handles and function-pointer typedefs, then resolve them through `LoadLibraryA`/`GetProcAddress`; it should not preserve generated names such as `dword_69BF80`, fixed addresses, or stale `0xffffffff` initialization.

The four resolved exports are `__cdecl`, not `__stdcall`: every indirect call is followed by caller stack cleanup. The two callbacks passed into the enumeration exports are independently `__stdcall`, proven by `V3ModuleEnumCallback` ending in `retn 0x10` and `V3ProcessEnumCallback` ending in `retn 0x14`. The process callback receives `processId`, three retained 32-bit vendor slots, and `VirusChecker *context`; the module callback receives `processId`, `moduleId`, `modulePath`, and context. This typed distinction replaces the provisional `void *callback`/all-stdcall draft.

Exact source order is position `10`, before UID0001NV load/reset/scan definitions at `20` and UID0001NW member/callback definitions at `30`. All six declarations are file-static in `VirusChecker.cpp`: no fixed-address global, raw `dword_` name, or header export is appropriate.
