## Summary

This is the exact one-dword read-only float literal at `0x0061a880`. Raw bytes are `00 00 80 3e`, little-endian `0x3e800000`, representing `0.25f`. It sits physically between the `FramePartPane` vtable-data child and the `GeneralPurposePanel` RTTI locator, but physical adjacency is not source ownership: the only direct address references are two `mulss` operands inside the RectBounds quarter-scale helper at `0x004b7af0-0x004b7b88`.

The source reconstruction must not invent a named `kRectBoundsQuarterScale` or `flt_RectBoundsQuarterScale` object. [UID:00022H][0x004b7af0-0x004b7c21.RectBoundsQuarterScaleHelpers](by-memory/0x004b7af0-0x004b7c21.RectBoundsQuarterScaleHelpers.md) emits the two source-shaped `0.25f` expressions and lets the MSVC/linker output pool them into this `.rdata` dword. Both formal channels therefore remain blank: this page documents retained binary material but contributes no independently authored declaration or definition.
