## IDA MCP Evidence

Checked on 2026-05-26 and refreshed with live IDA MCP evidence on 2026-06-15:

- `lookup_funcs` reports both starts as 11-byte functions.
- `lookup_funcs` also reports the adjacent successor thunk at `0x0049af53`, confirming the documented `0x0049af52` end byte is the last byte of this pair.
- `disasm` shows `sub ecx, 0A0h; jmp sub_49B170` and `sub ecx, 0A4h; jmp sub_49B170`.
- `analyze_function` decompiles the two bodies as direct `this - 160` and `this - 164` forwards into scalar deleting destructor `0x0049b170`; both bodies have no strings, no ordinary callers, no real callees beyond the tail jump, and complexity 1.
- `xrefs_to` ties the two thunks to `ControlPane` vtable data at `0x00617af8` and `0x00617b28`, with reuse cells at `0x00617b9c` and `0x00617bcc`.
- `xrefs_to 0x0049b170` reports the two thunk code refs at `0x0049af43` and `0x0049af4e`, plus primary destructor table refs at `0x00617a90` and `0x00617b34`.
- `xrefs_to 0x00617af8` and `0x00617b28` identify the `sub_4949E0` vtable-store/setup context at `0x00494a29` and `0x00494a33`.
- `make_signature_for_range 0x0049af3d-0x0049af52` produced unique IDA bytes `81 E9 A0 00 00 00 E9 28 02 00 00 81 E9 A4 00 00 00 E9 1D 02 00 00`.
- The same thunk targets also appear in some derived control vtables; that is destructor implementation reuse, not evidence that the thunk bodies are feature-owned source.

## Ownership Decision

These functions are ABI layout artifacts emitted for secondary and tertiary control-pane views. Reconstruct normal `ControlPane` inheritance/destructor behavior and keep these thunks out of handwritten source. `CANONICAL_OWNER:NONE`, blank emitter metadata, and blank reconstruction C++ are intentional because no standalone NexusTK source body exists for either wrapper.
