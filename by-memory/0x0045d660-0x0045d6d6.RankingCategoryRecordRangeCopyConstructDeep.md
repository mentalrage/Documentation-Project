## Exact Behavior

1. The SEH/security-cookie prologue installs `SEH_45D660` and records destination begin/current, ECX vector context, first, and last.
2. If `first == last`, no record is constructed and destination is returned unchanged.
3. Otherwise, `0x45d6a8` invokes UID0003X4 with destination in ECX and current source pushed as its explicit argument.

5. The loop repeats until source reaches `last`; EAX then returns the destination end.
