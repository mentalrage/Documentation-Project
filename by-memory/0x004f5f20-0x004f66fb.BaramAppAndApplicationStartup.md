## Evidence

- IDA MCP confirms `Application::Startup` at `0x004f5fb0-0x004f6490` and `Application::Shutdown` at `0x004f6490-0x004f66a5`.
- IDA MCP shows `Application::Startup` is called from the app shell at `0x004f5e78`.
- IDA MCP shows `Application::Startup` calls `Application::Initialize`, `IdleWatcher`, multiple image-library constructors, and other startup singleton constructors.
- IDA MCP reports no function containing `0x004f5f20`; disassembly proves a complete raw constructor body at `0x004f5f20-0x004f5f4e`, followed by two `0xcc` alignment bytes and the next promoted function at `0x004f5f50-0x004f5faf`.
- `xrefs_to 0x004f5f50` reports a data/callback target store at `0x004f63ec` inside `Application::Startup`; the function calls cursor-show helper `0x00557430`, allocator `0x004f4aa0`, and `TerminalPane::TerminalPane` at `0x0058af50`.
- `xrefs_to 0x004f5fb0` and `xrefs_to 0x004f6490` report `_WinMain@16` callsites and `BaramApp` vtable slots `0x0061d124` and `0x0061d128`.
- `xrefs_to 0x004f66b5` reports the secondary `BaramApp` vtable slot at `0x0061d138`; `xrefs_to 0x004f66c0` reports the thunk jump and primary vtable slot at `0x0061d118`.
- 2026-05-28 IDA MCP boundary check reports `sub_4F66C0` as `0x004f66c0-0x004f66fb`; the byte at `0x004f66fa` is the final immediate byte of `retn 4`, followed by `0xcc` alignment at `0x004f66fb-0x004f6700`.
- Exact `_WinMain@16` coverage is now separated into [UID:00019G][0x004f5c80-0x004f5f17.WinMain](by-memory/0x004f5c80-0x004f5f17.WinMain.md); this page begins after that confirmed function boundary.

## Reconstruction Notes

- Treat `0x004f5f20-0x004f5f4e` as a real unpromoted constructor-shaped body; the address is no longer just a Wave3 projection, but IDA still lacks a function object at the start.
- `Application::Startup` and `Application::Shutdown` are app-owned even though they construct/destroy many subsystem singletons.
- The startup function is strong evidence for source-folder dependencies, not for merging subsystem implementations into `Application.cpp`.
- The reconstructed C++ block is intentionally blank because this aggregate mixes two classes, raw constructor bytes, callback glue, real methods, thunks, and padding; exact child bodies remain the canonical reconstruction units.
