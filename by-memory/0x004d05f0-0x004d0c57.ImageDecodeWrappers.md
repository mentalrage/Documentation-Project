# 0x004d05f0-0x004d0c57 - Image Decode Wrappers

## Status

- Confidence: strong for wrapper behavior, callers, callees, and library boundary; medium for final original filename and because the legacy page filename/range still reflects earlier exclusive-end notation.
- Proposed owner: [UID:0000K3][ImageLoaders](by-file/ImageLoaders.md)
- Related library owner: [UID:0000KN][LibJPEG](by-file/LibJPEG.md)
- Evidence basis: read-only IDA MCP lookup/decompile/caller/callee checks on 2026-05-25 plus `simroot_v2` references in `ProfileStorage`, `UserLookPane`, `MiniMapRenderer`, and `MainMenuPane`.

- IDA MCP 2026-06-01 `lookup_funcs` reports exact function starts and sizes: `sub_4D05F0` size `0x133`, `sub_4D07B0` size `0x1f7`, `sub_4D09B0` size `0xda`, and `sub_4D0A90` size `0x1c8`.
- IDA MCP 2026-06-01 raw-byte audit shows the current row labels stop at the `retn` addresses for three functions: `0x004d0722`, `0x004d09a6`, `0x004d0a89`, and `0x004d0c57` are return instructions, followed by `0xcc` alignment. The filename/range is retained for this pass to avoid broad link churn, but any future boundary cleanup should migrate the wrapper span to include the final `retn` byte of the raw JPEG helper.
- IDA MCP 2026-06-01 raw-byte audit confirms alignment around the wrappers: after `sub_4D05F0` the padding begins at `0x004d0723`, after `sub_4D07B0` at `0x004d09a7`, after `sub_4D09B0` at `0x004d0a8a`, and after `sub_4D0A90` at `0x004d0c58`.

## Library Boundary

`DecodeJpfImageToTileContext` and `DecodeJpegBufferToTileContext` are NexusTK-owned wrappers over the statically linked [UID:0000VP][0x00401000-0x00414283.LibJpeg6bCore](by-memory/0x00401000-0x00414283.LibJpeg6bCore.md). They call `jpeg_std_error`-style setup at `0x00401000`, `jpeg_CreateDecompress` at `0x004011b0` with version `62`, in-memory source setup at `0x004e7030`, read-header/start/read-scanline/finish/destroy helpers documented in [UID:0000VQ][0x004011b0-0x004019e3.LibJpegDecompressApiFrontEnd](by-memory/0x004011b0-0x004019e3.LibJpegDecompressApiFrontEnd.md), then convert 24-bit RGB scanlines to RGB565.

Do not move IJG internals into `ImageLoaders.cpp`. The project-owned layer is the file-format wrapper and tile-context conversion policy.

`DecodeZpfFpfToTileContext` is similarly project-owned wrapper glue over a compressed format: it recognizes the outer `ZPF`, inflates into a temporary buffer, validates inner `FPF`, and copies the 16-bit body into the same tile-context shape.

## Callee Evidence

- IDA MCP 2026-06-01 `callees` shows `DecodeZpfFpfToTileContext` calls tile-context clear/allocation/finalizer helpers at `0x00458430`, `0x00458500`, and `0x00458560`, format/memory helpers `_strncmp`, `_memmove`, allocation/free helpers, and the decompression helper at `0x00414390`.
- IDA MCP 2026-06-01 `callees` shows both JPEG decode wrappers call the IJG setup/front-end sequence at `0x00401000`, `0x004011b0`, `0x00401290`, `0x004012b0`, `0x00401620`, `0x004016e0`, and `0x00401890`, plus the in-memory source helper at `0x004e7030`.
- IDA MCP 2026-06-01 `callees` shows `Decode8BitBmpToTileContext` calls palette/tile helpers at `0x00542cd0`, `0x00543020`, `0x004583d0`, and `0x00458500`, then copies rows with `_memmove`.

## Ownership Decision

Keep these helpers with [UID:0000K3][ImageLoaders](by-file/ImageLoaders.md) or a nearby legacy `ImageDecode.cpp` module. The callers span profile portraits, remote look panes, minimap data, and login background art, so the helper group is shared image/resource infrastructure rather than owned by any one feature.

`RECONSTRUCTABLE` is true because these are project-facing wrapper functions rather than IJG internals. `AUTOGEN_PARENT_UID` is left blank because [UID:0000K3][ImageLoaders](by-file/ImageLoaders.md) does not yet have a projected path in the validator registry.

Leave `RECONSTRUCTION_CPP` blank until the `EPFTileContext`, palette helper, and compressed-wrapper names are final enough to produce source-quality C++.
