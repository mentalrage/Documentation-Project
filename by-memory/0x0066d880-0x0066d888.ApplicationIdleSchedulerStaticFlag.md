## 2026-06-02 IDA Refresh

IDA MCP confirms:

- `byte_66D880` at `0x0066d880` has initial byte value `0x01`; the first dword at the address is `0x00000001`.
- Xrefs are exactly three data references inside `sub_4A6C40`: reads at `0x004a6c4f` and `0x004a6c77`, and clear/write at `0x004a6c92`.
- `0x004a6c40` is `sub_4A6C40`, range `0x004a6c40-0x004a6cdb`, with callers from `Application::RunMessageLoop` at `0x00464df7` and `0x00464e0e`.
- Decompilation of `0x004a6c40` shows the flag gates a timer refresh path: when set, the scheduler calls `0x00597e40`, later calls `0x00597e90`, clears `byte_66D880`, stores the current timer tick into helper offset `+0x2c`, and resets helper offset `+0x30` before normal elapsed-slice dispatch.
- Raw bytes at `0x0066d880-0x0066d890` are `01 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 1b 31 32 33 34 35 36`. This proves `0x0066d881-0x0066d887` is zero padding and `0x0066d888` begins the next table (`unk_66D888`, xref `0x004a8cfd`).
