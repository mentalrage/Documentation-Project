## Ownership Boundary

This page is an aggregate over the first half of the broader `0x004b9820-0x004ba81d` surface-helper neighborhood documented by [UID:0000OC][Surface](by-file/Surface.md). Surface remains the best source-placement hypothesis because every function in the range manipulates shared drawing-surface state, render callbacks, paint lifecycle state, or generic pixel buffers rather than feature-owned gameplay/UI state. The autogen parent is intentionally blank until this aggregate itself clears the strict child/parent assignment gate.

The individual recovered owners still matter as caller evidence: `FolderTreePane`, `MapPane`, ranking panes, fitting-room controls, Layer traversal, PhotoPane, and ScreenEffecter consumers all call into the surface helpers. That caller breadth is the reason these routines should not be migrated back into one of those feature files. The remaining uncertainty is narrower: whether the original source split was literally `Surface.cpp`, a `GrafPort` method group, or a smaller shared blit/presentation helper file.
