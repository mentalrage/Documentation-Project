## Behavior

This method handles user-requested skipping from the first startup movie segment to the next segment. It checks the skip guard at `+0x110` and current video progress through the `VideoPlayerPane` state near `+0xfc`; if the pane is still eligible to advance, it computes the second segment pointer from the AUTOBUF base plus the saved first-segment size, marks the guard, and calls `0x005c0110` to open the next memory-backed Bink segment.
