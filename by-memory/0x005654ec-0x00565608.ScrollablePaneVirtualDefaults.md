## Notes

These late virtuals are part of `ScrollablePane` even though they are far from the main `0x0055e660` constructor/layout cluster. The intervening addresses contain `TextEditPane` methods, so avoid documenting one broad `0x0055e660-0x00565608` class range as if it were contiguous ownership.

2026-05-26 IDA MCP recheck confirms the immediately preceding `0x005654ec` and `0x005654f7` thunks are secondary/tertiary adjustors that subtract `0xa0` or `0xa4` and jump to `0x00565510`. They sit after [UID:0001H2][0x00564710-0x005654ec.ScrollVolumePane](by-memory/0x00564710-0x005654ec.ScrollVolumePane.md) raw reset code, but belong to this `ScrollablePane` destructor tail.

2026-05-28 IDA MCP boundary review moved the page start to include those two adjustor thunks and corrected `IsScrollable` to end at `0x00565608`. The spans `0x00565502-0x00565510` and `0x00565608-0x00565610` are padding.
