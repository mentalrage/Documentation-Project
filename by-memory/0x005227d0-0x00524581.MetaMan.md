The island owns metadata lifecycle:

- startup/load: constructor and `LoadMetaDat`;
- network sync: `LoadMetaDatAndRequestSync` and `HandleMetaPacket`;
- lookup API: `FindEntry`, `LoadStatValues`, and lower direct helpers;
- persistence: private dirty-cache writer at `0x005237d0`;
- teardown: destructor, base destroy helper, and scalar deleting destructor.
