- Current `source-3/simroot_v2/class_MapPane.cpp` emits this as `MapPane::DrawSpriteAtPosition`, but the IDA caller set is broader than map-specific rendering: eight direct callers at `0x004f12b4`, `0x0050978f`, `0x00549986`, `0x0055b27b`, `0x0055b45f`, `0x0055b592`, `0x0055b930`, and `0x0055b9c7`.
- Callees are shared surface/render infrastructure: [UID:000165][0x004b9820-0x004ba24a.SurfacePaintHelpers](by-memory/0x004b9820-0x004ba24a.SurfacePaintHelpers.md) at `0x004b9820`, `EndPaint` at `0x004b98c0`, and callback slot `dword_69B3E8` from [UID:0000TN][SurfaceRenderCallbackTable](by-global/SurfaceRenderCallbackTable.md).
- IDA decompilation shows two major paths: DirectDraw surface blit via the surface vtable slot at `+0x1c`, and callback-based software/composition blit through `dword_69B3E8(a2 + 0x1c, srcRect, dstRect, mode, 0, 0)`.
- The body reads and restores float state at `this + 0x90` from the source object, so it behaves like a generic surface composition operation rather than MapPane gameplay logic.
