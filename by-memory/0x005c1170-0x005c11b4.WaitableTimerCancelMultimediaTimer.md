## Behavior

This helper cancels the active multimedia timer without destroying the `WaitableTimer` object:

- enters the critical section at `this + 0x08`;
- clears byte field `this + 0x20`;
- calls `timeKillEvent(this + 0x24)`;
- closes the callback-gate event at `this + 0x28`;
- clears `this + 0x24` and `this + 0x28`;
- resets the completion event at `this + 0x04`;
- leaves the critical section.

Unlike the destructor/cleanup bodies, this helper does not delete the critical section and does not close the persistent completion event at `this + 0x04`.
