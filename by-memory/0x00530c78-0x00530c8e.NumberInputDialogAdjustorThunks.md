## Ownership Decision

These are compiler/vtable support thunks for `NumberInputDialog`, not source-level feature methods. They should be recognized as reviewed compiler glue rather than left as bad/missing generated data or reconstructed as handwritten methods.

The scalar deleting destructor at `0x00530c90-0x00530cf7` remains reconstructable; only these `this`-adjusting wrappers are excluded from source reconstruction.
