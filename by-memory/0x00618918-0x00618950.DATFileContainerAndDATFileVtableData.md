## Reconstruction Notes

`DATFileContainer` vtable data should be regenerated by the mapped-archive container declaration in the DAT manager source family. `DATFile` vtable data should be regenerated by the concrete DAT-backed stream implementation that derives from the abstract `File` interface. Do not fold `DATFileContainer` into the stream interface merely because its one-slot vtable is adjacent to `DATFile`.
