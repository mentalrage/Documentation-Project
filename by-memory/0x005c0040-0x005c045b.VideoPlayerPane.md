## IDA MCP Evidence

Checked on 2026-05-24:

- IDA confirms all listed starts and exact function sizes through `0x005c045b`.
- `0x005c0040` is called by `LogoPlayerPane::LogoPlayerPane`.
- `0x005c0110` is called by `LogoPlayerPane::LogoPlayerPane` and `LogoPlayerPane::AdvanceToNextSegment`.

- Playback virtuals have vtable data xrefs in the `0x61d0xx` and `0x6310xx` tables.
- `0x005c03a0` is reached by two adjustor thunks at `0x005c037b` and `0x005c0386`.
