## IDA Evidence

Checked on 2026-05-24:

- `lookup_funcs` confirms `0x005975e0` size `0x1f` and `0x00597600` size `0x0d`.

- Caller evidence for both wrappers is broad across UI, object, map, effect, and timer-pane code. That breadth matches a base timer-handler wrapper surface, not `InterfaceEfx`-local source ownership.

Checked from raw IDA export and executable bytes on 2026-06-02:

- Raw IDA decompilation for `0x005975e0` is the direct forwarder `sub_597910((_DWORD *)dword_67AB80, (int)this, a2, a3, a4, a5)`.
- Raw IDA export for `0x00597600` reports name `sub_597600`, size `13`, signature `int __thiscall(void *this)`, and a single outgoing call to `0x00597a10` / `sub_597A10`.
- Raw IDA decompilation for `0x00597600` is the direct forwarder `sub_597A10((_DWORD *)dword_67AB80, (int)this)`.
- Raw IDA `xrefs_to` counts show `0x005975e0` has 163 incoming references and `0x00597600` has 45 incoming references. Sampled callers include fitting-room/scroll controls, ranking scroll panes, InterfaceEfx, logo panes, help panes, inventory/collection scroll panes, SoundManager redbook playback, TerminalPane, TimerPane, and many other timer users.

## Ownership Decision

Do not keep `0x00597600` as an `InterfaceEfx` source helper. `InterfaceEfx` is a confirmed caller/consumer through its scheduled effect lifetime, but the body is the same generic timer-handler removal wrapper used by many timer-owning classes.

Do not revive older `FittingRoomDialog::ScheduleTimer` naming for `0x005975e0`. Fitting-room pages have many calls to this helper, but the raw export caller count and `g_pTimerMgr` forwarder body prove the source owner is the generic timer-handler wrapper surface.
