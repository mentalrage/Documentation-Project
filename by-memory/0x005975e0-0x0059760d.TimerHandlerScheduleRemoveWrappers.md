- `lookup_funcs` confirms `0x005975e0` size `0x1f` and `0x00597600` size `0x0d`.

- Caller evidence for both wrappers is broad across UI, object, map, effect, and timer-pane code. That breadth matches a base timer-handler wrapper surface, not `InterfaceEfx`-local source ownership.

## Ownership Decision

Do not keep `0x00597600` as an `InterfaceEfx` source helper. `InterfaceEfx` is a confirmed caller/consumer through its scheduled effect lifetime, but the body is the same generic timer-handler removal wrapper used by many timer-owning classes.

The adjacent cancellation wrappers at `0x00597610` and `0x00597630` remain documented in [UID:0001K9][0x00597610-0x00597645.TimerHandlerWrappers](by-memory/0x00597610-0x00597645.TimerHandlerWrappers.md).
