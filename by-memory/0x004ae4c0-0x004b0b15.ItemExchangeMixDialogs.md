## Ownership Decision

This cluster is item exchange/mixing dialog UI. It belongs under `ui/dialogs/ItemDialogs.cpp`, [UID:0000LO][MyItemListPane](by-file/MyItemListPane.md), or a nearby `MixItemDialog.cpp`, separate from inventory pane rendering and item image libraries.

Do not extend this ownership backward into `ExchangeDialog` or forward across the whole tail island. `ExchangeDialog`, `ExchangeAlertPane`, and `ExchangeMoneyEditControlPane` are documented in [UID:00014V][0x004b0490-0x004b0ba5.ExchangeDialogTail](by-memory/0x004b0490-0x004b0ba5.ExchangeDialogTail.md); only `dword_69B328` and `dword_69B32C` item/mix singleton rows remain here.

The destructor adjustor thunks in the `0x004b08a1+` strip are compiler-generated layout glue for the confirmed item-dialog vtables. Preserve their evidence for binary matching, but model them through C++ inheritance/destructor layout rather than handwritten source methods.
