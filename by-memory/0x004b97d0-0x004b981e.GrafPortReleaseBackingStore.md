## Behavior

`GrafPort::ReleaseBackingStore` releases the two per-instance backing-store forms owned by GrafPort.

- Reads `m_usesSoftwareBuffer` at `this+0x94`.
- If the software-buffer flag is set and `this+0x98` is non-null, frees the heap buffer through `j_j_j___free_base` and clears `this+0x98`.
- Reads the retained DirectDraw surface pointer at `this+0x18`.
- If the surface pointer is non-null, `g_pDirectX` is non-null, and `g_pDirectX->m_pDirectDraw` at `g_pDirectX+0x04` is non-null, calls vtable slot `+8` on the surface pointer and clears `this+0x18`.

The DirectDraw call is the COM `Release` slot on the retained surface. `g_pDirectX` and its `m_pDirectDraw` field are dependencies used to guard the release path; DirectX does not own this helper.
