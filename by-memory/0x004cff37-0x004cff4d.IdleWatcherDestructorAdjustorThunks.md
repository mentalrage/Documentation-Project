## RTTI And Vtable Evidence

- RTTI hierarchy PMDs place EventHandler at `+0xa0` and TimerHandler at `+0xa4`, exactly matching the two subtraction constants.
- The secondary vtable view at `0x0061b5b8` otherwise matches Pane's EventHandler view; its destructor slot differs only to reach this adjustor.
- The tertiary view at `0x0061b5e8` contains this destructor adjustor followed by the source-authored IdleWatcher `OnTimer` override at `0x0061b5ec`.
- Constructor, ordinary destructor, and scalar destructor install/restore these views as compiler ABI state.

## No-Code Proof

- A normal virtual `~IdleWatcher()` plus the accepted `Pane, Singleton<IdleWatcher>` inheritance graph causes the compiler to regenerate the required secondary-view adjustors.
- Hand-porting the subtraction/jump bodies would expose ABI glue as source, duplicate compiler output, and create nonportable manual deleting-destructor entries.
- There is no body-only example or covered-by marker because this page is reviewed compiler output, not source represented elsewhere at the same range.

## Boundaries And Negative Evidence

- The source-authored OnTimer function ends exactly at `0x004cff37`, where the first thunk begins.
- The second thunk ends at `0x004cff4d`; `0x004cff4d-0x004cff50` is three bytes of `0xcc` alignment before the scalar body.
- No call/data route identifies either start as a human-callable method. Their only data routes are the matching secondary/tertiary destructor slots.
- Historical aggregate wording that grouped these thunks with source destruction is superseded. They do not receive owner/emitter UID00006C and must not appear in generated `IdleWatcher.cpp`.
