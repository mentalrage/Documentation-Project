## Range

- Entity kind: `vtable/string-data`
- Disposition: reconstructable source-declared/generated-binary data.

## Summary

This `.rdata` span contains `RingBuffer`, `RingBufferIterator`, and `ScreenPane` complete-object-locator pointers/vtables, followed by screen window, screenshot, and cursor-resource strings.
