These helpers duplicate the story/history construction branches inside `MainMenuPane::ActivateMenuItem`. IDA reports no direct callers or xrefs to either helper start, and a 2026-05-28 raw pointer/immediate scan found no hidden table references to either start. Treat them as retained duplicate launch wrappers unless a later callback path is proven.
