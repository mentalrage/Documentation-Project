## Behavior

This method handles mouse selection and drag state for the editor. It rejects input unless the editor's active-selection flag is enabled, converts mouse coordinates into text positions, tracks click timing and repeated clicks, handles press/drag/release cases, updates the global scratch selection word at `0x0069be04`, moves the selection anchor/end points, and invalidates or autoscrolls the selected region.
