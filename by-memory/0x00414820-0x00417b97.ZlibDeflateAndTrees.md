## Range Role

This range is the deflate half of the bundled zlib island after public setup/reset and before inflate-block decoding. It contains the zlib `deflate.c` worker state machine plus the `trees.c` Huffman/bit-output helpers used by deflate.

Do not treat this range as NexusTK product code. It should stay under the bundled zlib support module, even though current generated output materializes only a few named globals such as `InitializeLongestMatch_00414C30.cpp`.
