## Behavior

Optionally reports bytes written, writes a NUL byte at the current cursor, detaches the buffer, clears capacity/cursor, restores valid state, and returns the previous valid/writable state.

This is the detach/finalize half of the [UID:00013J][0x004a55c0-0x004a55de.EncoderInitialize](by-memory/0x004a55c0-0x004a55de.EncoderInitialize.md) attach/reset pair. `Initialize` stores caller-owned output storage, resets `m_writePos`, sets `m_isValid`, and preserves `m_useBigEndian`; `Finalize` saves the previous valid state, optionally reports the cursor, writes the trailing NUL at `m_outputBuffer[m_writePos]`, clears `m_outputBuffer`/`m_outputCapacity`/`m_writePos`, resets `m_isValid`, and also does not free caller-owned storage.
