# 0x00461fe0-0x00462114 AesKeySchedule128

## Behavior

Copies a 16-byte key from the pointer stored at `0x0067a87c` into the expanded-key buffer at `0x0067a880`, then expands it through 44 32-bit words / 176 bytes for 10 AES rounds plus the initial round key.

Every fourth word applies RotWord, SubWord through the forward S-box at `0x00610fe8`, and XORs the round constant from `0x006111e8`.
