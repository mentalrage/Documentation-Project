- `lookup_funcs` confirms function starts and exclusive ends for `0x004b5c40-0x004b5cbc`, `0x004b5cc0-0x004b5ce5`, `0x004b5cf0-0x004b5d14`, `0x004b5d20-0x004b5ddf`, `0x004b5de0-0x004b5e75`, `0x004b5e80-0x004b5ebc`, and `0x004b5ec0-0x004b5efc`.

- `xrefs_to 0x004b5c40` reports many calls from folder-tree compare/copy/sort helpers at `0x004b3350`, `0x004b4240`, `0x004b4520`, and `0x004b4fd0`.
- `xrefs_to 0x004b5cc0`, `0x004b5d20`, and `0x004b5e80` report callers inside `TreeStorage<FolderTreePane::TreeElem>` insertion support at `0x004b3d50`.
- `xrefs_to 0x004b5cf0`, `0x004b5de0`, and `0x004b5ec0` report callers inside directory-entry vector helpers at `0x004b3fb0`, plus `0x004b5cf0` and `0x004b5ec0` are also called by the SpelledPane entry-vector insert helper at `0x00572fd0`.
- Decompilation identifies the record widths: 36-byte records for folder-tree nodes and 12-byte records for directory/entry records.

## Source Placement

Keep these helpers linked to the folder-tree source reconstruction because most callers are local to [UID:000157][0x004b3350-0x004b5c3f.FolderTreePaneTreeAndSortHelpers](by-memory/0x004b3350-0x004b5c3f.FolderTreePaneTreeAndSortHelpers.md).
