`0x00556100` is a file-local `void` vote sender taking `const wchar_t *targetName` and a boolean promote flag:

- relies on compiler-generated range checks around indexed fixed arrays rather than handwritten range-failure source.

The aggregate itself has no behavior beyond indexing these children and the five-byte `0xcc` alignment span. It must not emit a duplicate wrapper, covered-by body, or Empty Emitter Marker.
