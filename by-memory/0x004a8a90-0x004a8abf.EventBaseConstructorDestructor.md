## Exact Bytes And Source Semantics

- The target is exactly 47 bytes: constructor `0x004a8a90-0x004a8ab0` (`0x20` bytes) followed immediately by destructor `0x004a8ab0-0x004a8abf` (`0x0f` bytes). There is no internal gap or nested child range.
- Constructor anchors are the [UID:00036Z][0x004f4a80-0x004f4a89.LObjectConstructor](by-memory/0x004f4a80-0x004f4a89.LObjectConstructor.md) call at `0x004a8a9a`, Event vptr store `0x006196a4` at `0x004a8a9f`, and byte-`0xff` store at `0x004a8aa7`.
- Destructor anchors are the Event vptr store at `0x004a8ab0`, byte-`0xff` store at `0x004a8ab6`, and tail jump to [UID:000370][0x004f4a90-0x004f4a97.LObjectDestructorBody](by-memory/0x004f4a90-0x004f4a97.LObjectDestructorBody.md) at `0x004a8aba`.
- Source-visible semantics are `m_type(kEventNone)` and `m_type = kEventNone`. The LObject calls, vptr stores, return-this convention, and tail-call lowering are compiler-generated and intentionally absent from formal source.
- `m_type` is an unsigned byte at Event `+0x04`. The classifiers use byte arithmetic and unsigned `setbe`; treating the field as signed would make sentinel `0xff` compare incorrectly against low event families.
- `kEventNone = 0xff` is the accepted descriptive source name. Constructor, ordinary destructor, scalar wrapper, and temporary Event cleanup all restore this value, and no dispatcher classifier accepts it.
