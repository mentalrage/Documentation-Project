## Behavior

This raw helper replaces a copied-text entry. It first fetches the existing entry slot through `0x004f3dc0`, frees and clears any existing heap-backed wide-string buffer, removes one entry at the requested index through `0x004f3d60`, allocates and copies the replacement UTF-16 string, then reinserts the copied pointer through `0x004f3c00`.

## Boundary Notes

IDA does not currently model `0x00573bb0` as a function start. Raw disassembly shows a complete method-shaped body ending with `retn 8` at `0x00573c35`, immediately before the `0x00573c38` secondary-base destructor thunk.
