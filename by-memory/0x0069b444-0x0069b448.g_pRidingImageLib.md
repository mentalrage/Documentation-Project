## Evidence Notes

- Live IDA MCP on 2026-06-05 reports item head `0x0069b444`, size `4`, segment `.data`, name `dword_69B444`, bytes `ff ff ff ff`, and 20 direct xrefs.
- `0x004dc690-0x004dc722` is the singleton constructor body. It stores `this` at `0x004dc6c4`, clears the slot on the constructor guard/fallback path at `0x004dc6cb`, installs the `RidingImageLib` vtable, calls `0x004dc420` to load riding definitions, and loads `RIDINGS.EPF` at `0x004dc70c`.
- IDA callers show `0x004dc690` has one direct startup caller at `0x004f60ed` inside `0x004f5fb0-0x004f6490`.
- `0x004dc730-0x004dc7d8` is the ordinary destructor body and clears `dword_69B444` at `0x004dc7bc` after freeing riding image resources and base image-library state.
- `0x004e5bf0-0x004e5bfb` is a tiny cleanup helper whose whole body is `dword_69B444 = 0`.
- `0x004e68b0-0x004e6981` is the scalar deleting destructor body and clears `dword_69B444` at `0x004e6943` before conditional object deletion.
- `0x004f6490-0x004f66a5` is called by `_WinMain@16` at `0x004f5e8e`; its shutdown sweep reads `dword_69B444` at `0x004f6612` and invokes the singleton deleting destructor when non-null.
- Touched state: five direct lifecycle writes/clears and fifteen direct reads. The reads are concentrated in riding-aware sprite lookup/composition paths plus the application shutdown sweep.
- This slot stays distinct from the adjacent riding definition table at `0x0069b430`, the old human singleton at `0x0069b43c`, the monster singleton at `0x0069b440`, and the adjacent effect/new-human/image-library globals.
