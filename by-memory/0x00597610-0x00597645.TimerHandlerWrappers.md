# 0x00597610-0x00597645 TimerHandlerWrappers

## Status

- Disposition: reconstructable NexusTK project code.
- Entity kind: timer-manager wrapper helpers misattributed as `SoundManager` in active generated output.
- Current/generated owner evidence: active `class_SoundManager.cpp` still emits these as `SoundManager::CancelTimer` and `SoundManager::CancelPendingTimersExceptEvent`; IDA body/caller checks support `TimerHandler`/`TimerMgr`.

## IDA MCP Evidence

Checked on 2026-05-23 and rechecked on 2026-05-24: IDA confirms starts at `0x00597610` and `0x00597630`, both size `0x16`. IDA decompilation shows both call through global `dword_67AB80`, now documented as [UID:0000SI][g_pTimerMgr](by-global/g_pTimerMgr.md). `0x00597610` has broad callers outside the audio cluster, including [UID:00007B][LivingObjectPane](by-class/LivingObjectPane.md) animation and movement helpers at `0x0053a900`, `0x0053b700`, `0x0053bf40`, `0x0053bf80`, and `0x005a5a90`; `0x00597630` is called from `LivingObjectPane::SetSpriteConfig` at `0x0053a96d`. The callee targets are the timer-manager removal helpers at `0x00597b80` and `0x00597ca0`.

## Ownership Decision

Do not use these wrappers as evidence for `audio/SoundManager.cpp`. They belong with the generic [UID:0000F0][TimerHandler](by-class/TimerHandler.md) / [UID:0000F1][TimerMgr](by-class/TimerMgr.md) scheduler cluster and are included in [UID:0001K6][0x00597570-0x005986da.TimerMgrAndTimerQueue](by-memory/0x00597570-0x005986da.TimerMgrAndTimerQueue.md). SoundManager remains a caller/derived timer owner, not the source owner for the wrappers.

The adjacent schedule/remove-all wrappers at `0x005975e0` and `0x00597600` are documented separately in [UID:0001K8][0x005975e0-0x0059760d.TimerHandlerScheduleRemoveWrappers](by-memory/0x005975e0-0x0059760d.TimerHandlerScheduleRemoveWrappers.md).
