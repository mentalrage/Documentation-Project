## Detailed Behavior

`ScanMusicDirectory` is the local-file side of SoundManager music selection. It walks configured loose music folders through Win32 enumeration, builds candidate path strings with the audio path separators documented by [UID:0000NV][SoundManager](by-file/SoundManager.md), filters file names through `g_strMusicExtension`, and stores accepted paths in the SoundManager path-vector storage used later by stream playback.

This method complements the DAT-backed music path documented in [UID:0001I7][0x005797b0-0x0057a25c.SoundManagerMusicSelection](by-memory/0x005797b0-0x0057a25c.SoundManagerMusicSelection.md). Zone music selection checks `%08d.LST`, `%08d.LSR`, and `%08d.MP3` through the DAT archive API, while this scanner discovers user/local loose files through `FindFirstFileA` and `FindNextFileA`.

The path-storage relationship is audio-local rather than generic container ownership. [UID:0000DI][SoundPathVector](by-class/SoundPathVector.md) identifies a three-pointer audio path vector, and [UID:0001ID][0x0057b860-0x0057bf6e.SoundPathVector](by-memory/0x0057b860-0x0057bf6e.SoundPathVector.md) ties grow/reallocate/destructor helpers to SoundManager playlist and scanned-path storage.
