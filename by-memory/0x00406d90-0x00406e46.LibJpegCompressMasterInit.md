## Range Role

This range is `jinit_compress_master`, IJG libjpeg's compression-side module coordinator. It initializes the compression master-control, color conversion, downsampling, preprocessing, DCT, entropy, coefficient, main, and marker writer modules, then starts the first compression pass.

The range begins at `0x00406d90`, immediately after the compression marker writer, and ends at `0x00406e46`. The next documented range at `0x00406e50` is the no-backing-store memory backend.
