- `lookup_funcs` reports real functions at `0x00582200`, `0x00582210`, and `0x00582230` with sizes `0xc`, `0x1d`, and `0x1a`.

- Vtable data refs place `GetPosition` at `0x0062d5c0`, `Seek` at `0x0062d5c4`, and `Read` at `0x0062d5cc`.
