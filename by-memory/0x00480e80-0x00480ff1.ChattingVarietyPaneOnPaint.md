Before drawing the label, it calls the [UID:000162][0x004b95e0-0x004b969f.GrafPortDrawStateAccessors](by-memory/0x004b95e0-0x004b969f.GrafPortDrawStateAccessors.md) background-color setter `sub_4B9680(this, 128)`.
