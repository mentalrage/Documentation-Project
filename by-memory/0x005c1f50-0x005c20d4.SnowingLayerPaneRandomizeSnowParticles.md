This method randomizes and refills SnowingLayerPane particle records. The target is a virtual Snowing method, not a free helper, not a table initializer, and not a no-route raw island.

Best-supported behavior statement: the method first applies a full 200-particle random/`OffsetRect` pass; then, unless nonzero inherited `m_currentFrame` suppresses it, it shifts the rolling list by 20 entries and creates 20 new leading particles within inherited `m_weatherBounds`.
