## Evidence

- IDA MCP `py_eval` on 2026-06-07 reads both dwords as `0xffffffff`.
- IDA MCP `xrefs_to` reports `0x0069bf6c` refs at `0x005a4fba` in `sub_5A4F70` and `0x005a686f` in `sub_5A5BD0`.
- IDA MCP `xrefs_to` reports `0x0069bf70` refs at `0x005a4fff` in `sub_5A4F70` and `0x005a68a4` in `sub_5A5BD0`.
- IDA MCP disassembly of `0x005a4f70-0x005a500f` shows the helper writes opcode byte `0x2d`, subtype byte `0`, sends a two-byte packet through [UID:0000Q5][g_packetSender](by-global/g_packetSender.md), and stores the helper argument into one of these two dwords according to [UID:0000SW][g_useEpfAssets](by-global/g_useEpfAssets.md) / historical `byte_66DA97`.
- IDA MCP callers for `0x005a4f70` are old-HUD/UI action paths at `0x004cf6ef`, `0x004cfa61`, and `0x00507721`.

## Assignment Gate
