## Ownership Decision

These are item context-menu dialogs and private row-list widgets and should be migrated together. They are later in memory than exchange/mix item dialogs but share the item-action UI domain.

The filename range is now best treated as a historical index page rather than one exact contiguous class range: it includes `ServerItemMenuDialog`, `ServerItemMenuItemList`, and `ClientItemMenuDialog`, while the adjacent `ClientItemMenuItemList` begins immediately after at `0x0051c470`.
