## Behavior

`0x004b11a0` builds and sends a field-map request packet for a selected field entry:

- multiplies the selected index by the 0x94-byte field-entry record size;
- initializes a packet buffer with opcode `0x3f`;
- writes three entry values from offsets equivalent to record `+0x8a`, `+0x90`, and `+0x8c`;
- queues/sends seven packet bytes through the global packet sender at `dword_67a7ec`;
- marks the request-in-flight byte at owner offset `0x95fe`.

The helper is invoked by click/enter/cancel handling and guards duplicate requests through the request-in-flight flag.
