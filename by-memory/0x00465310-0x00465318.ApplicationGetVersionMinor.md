## Behavior

This tiny accessor returns the 16-bit field at `this + 0x842`.

The previous `GetVersionMinor` name is now provisional and likely stale: [UID:0002H6][0x00465430-0x0046564c.ApplicationValidateLicense](by-memory/0x00465430-0x0046564c.ApplicationValidateLicense.md) writes the low 16 bits of `VS_FIXEDFILEINFO.dwFileVersionLS` here after a successful executable version-resource query, and clears it to zero on query failure. The field is better understood as the stored executable version low word than as a generic version-minor value.
