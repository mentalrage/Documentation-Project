## Behavior

This helper shrinks a `RectBounds` record in place:

- adds `dx` to `left`;
- subtracts `dx` from `right`;
- adds `dy` to `top`;
- subtracts `dy` from `bottom`;
- if the horizontal axis inverts, collapses `left` and `right` to their midpoint;
- if the vertical axis inverts, collapses `top` and `bottom` to their midpoint.

This is the member-style counterpart to the free geometry helper at `0x004b7e30`.
