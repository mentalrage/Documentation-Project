## Behavior

Initializes the multimedia timer period used by the scheduler. It queries timer device capabilities, chooses a period no lower than one millisecond and no higher than the reported maximum, stores the chosen period in the TimerMgr object, calls `timeBeginPeriod`, samples `timeGetTime`, stores the sampled tick in the object, and mirrors it into `dword_69BE10` and `dword_69BE0C`.
