## Summary

This range is a raw, unmodeled ThreadMan watch-list helper island between [UID:0001K3][0x00597100-0x005971ad.ThreadManWorkerEntry](by-memory/0x00597100-0x005971ad.ThreadManWorkerEntry.md) and [UID:0001K4][0x005974e0-0x005974eb.ClearThreadManSingleton](by-memory/0x005974e0-0x005974eb.ClearThreadManSingleton.md). The outer bytes are now split: `0x005971ad-0x005971b0` and `0x005974da-0x005974e0` are ignored `0xcc` alignment padding, while this exact child contains the source-shaped helper bodies.

The helpers operate on `ThreadMan + 0x10`, the `m_watchList` pointer documented by [UID:0001WA][ThreadManLayout](by-type/by-struct/ThreadManLayout.md), and on the 20-byte [UID:0001WB][ThreadManWatchRecord](by-type/by-struct/ThreadManWatchRecord.md) layout. They mirror worker-loop operations for message ids documented by [UID:0001SR][ThreadManMessageId](by-type/by-enum/ThreadManMessageId.md).
