# 0x00631850-0x0063211c LibJPEG 6b Read-Only Tables

## Summary

- Address range: `0x00631850-0x0063211c`
- Entity kind: third-party static read-only table/constant island
- Disposition: reconstructable third-party static-embed data.
- Rebuild handling: regenerated by compiling/linking bundled IJG libjpeg 6b source files; do not attach to zlib, Browser, MSVC runtime, or NexusTK product modules.
- Parent/source owner: [UID:0000KN][LibJPEG](by-file/LibJPEG.md)

This `.rdata` child is the IJG libjpeg 6b read-only table and DCT constant island immediately after the libjpeg message pointer table. The range begins at `0x00631850`, carries libjpeg data through the pooled floating-point constants ending at `0x0063211c`, and stops exactly where the zlib 1.1.4 CRC table begins.

The prior classification of `0x00631850-0x0063211c` as zlib deflate/tree constants is superseded. Current B009 MCP/source audit proves this whole interval is libjpeg data: `jcparam.c` standard quantization/Huffman defaults, `jmemmgr.c` pool slop arrays, `jutils.c` `jpeg_natural_order`, Huffman extend tables, inverse and forward DCT scaling tables, ordered-dither/RGB-order data, and floating DCT constants.
