## Behavior

This is the ordinary non-deleting destructor for `ObjectList`. The scalar deleting destructor at [UID:0001D4][0x00537290-0x005372c8.ObjectListScalarDeletingDestructor](by-memory/0x00537290-0x005372c8.ObjectListScalarDeletingDestructor.md) calls this body and then optionally deletes the object.

Cleanup order observed in IDA:

1. Restore the `ObjectList` vtable.
2. Destroy every row `List` stored in the `+0x24` row-list array, then free that array.
3. Destroy every row `List` stored in the `+0x28` extended row-list array, then free that array.
4. Destroy flat/cell lists at `+0x14` and `+0x18`.
5. Destroy row-list arrays at `+0x1c` and `+0x20`, including every row `List` in each array, then free both arrays.
6. Destroy global object-list tiers at `+0x2c`, `+0x30`, conditional `+0x34`, `+0x38`, `+0x3c`, and `+0x40`.
7. Tail-call or fall through to the `LObject` destructor at `0x004f4a90`.
