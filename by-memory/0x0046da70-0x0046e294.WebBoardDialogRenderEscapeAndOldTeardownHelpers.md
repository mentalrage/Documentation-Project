- Vtable/data inspection reports `0x00613620 -> 0x0046dbd0`, `0x0061362c -> 0x0046dd00`, and `0x00613630 -> 0x0046dea0`; these entries sit in the same WebBoardDialog vtable area as the previously documented close/input/packet slots.

- Decompilation of `0x0046dbd0`, `0x0046dd00`, and `0x0046dea0` confirms browser/dialog layout and render behavior rather than RankingDialog behavior.
