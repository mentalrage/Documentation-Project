## Behavior

The routine blends two RGB565 pixels without touching global state or calling other functions. It computes the inverse weight as `32 - weight`, keeps the green lane separate with mask `0x07e0`, keeps red/blue together with mask `0xf81f`, multiplies each lane group by the source and inverse weights, shifts the accumulated values right by five, and recombines the lane groups into one RGB565 result.

This is project-owned render math rather than CRT/compiler glue. Final source should keep it as a small free helper under [UID:0000NT][SoftwareBlend16](by-file/SoftwareBlend16.md) near the adjacent packed-pair helper; C++ remains blank until final helper naming and surrounding typedefs reach the 95/95 final-code bar.
