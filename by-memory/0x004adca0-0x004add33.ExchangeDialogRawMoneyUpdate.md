## Behavior

The raw body updates the exchange money display from a packet. It reads:

- side byte at `packet+2`;
- big-endian amount at `packet+3`;
- control `6` for the local money edit control when side is local;
- control `9` for the remote money display when side is remote.

If the local amount is zero, it calls `ExchangeDialog::SetExchangeAmount(0)`. It formats the amount with `"%u"` and writes the resulting wide text into the selected control.
