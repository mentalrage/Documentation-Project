## Behavior

Adjusts one axis of a pair of rectangles according to a 3x3 anchor index and caller-supplied offset. The caller passes pointers to the low/high edges of two rectangles. The helper:

- maps anchor cases into start, center, or end alignment for the axis;
- compares the requested offset against the source/destination span difference;
- modifies the input edge pointers in place to keep the two rectangles aligned/clipped according to the selected anchor band.
