## Behavior Summary

The helper allocates `0x280` bytes and constructs either [UID:00003B][CreateUserDialogPane](by-class/CreateUserDialogPane.md) or [UID:00009E][NewUserDialogPane2](by-class/NewUserDialogPane2.md), depending on the menu layout flag. In the `byte_66DA97 == 1` branch it first closes the active login dialog singleton at `dword_69B484`, constructs the older `CreateUserDialogPane`, and then plays a sound/music action through `dword_67A7D0`. The alternate branch constructs `NewUserDialogPane2` without the singleton close or sound call.
