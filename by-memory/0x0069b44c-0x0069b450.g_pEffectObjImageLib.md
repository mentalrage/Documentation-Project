## Evidence Notes

- Live IDA MCP on 2026-06-05 reports item head `0x0069b44c`, size `4`, segment `.data`, name `dword_69B44C`, bytes `ff ff ff ff`, and 12 direct xrefs.
- `0x004ddf60-0x004de04e` is the singleton constructor body. It stores `this` at `0x004ddf9f`, clears the slot on the constructor guard/fallback path at `0x004ddfa6`, installs the `EffectObjImageLib` vtable, initializes protected effect descriptor/pixmap arrays, loads effect tables through `0x004de7d0`, loads `EFFECT.EPF` at `0x004de007`, and validates loaded frame coverage.
- IDA callers show `0x004ddf60` has one direct startup caller at `0x004f6113` inside `0x004f5fb0-0x004f6490`.
- `0x004de050-0x004de189` is the ordinary destructor body and clears `dword_69B44C` at `0x004de167` after freeing effect descriptor frame arrays, EPF metadata, protected array storage, and base image-library state.
- `0x004e5b70-0x004e5b7b` is a tiny cleanup helper whose whole body is `dword_69B44C = 0`.
- `0x004e62f0-0x004e6456` is the scalar deleting destructor body and clears `dword_69B44C` at `0x004e640e` before conditional object deletion.
- `0x004f6490-0x004f66a5` is called by `_WinMain@16` at `0x004f5e8e`; its shutdown sweep reads `dword_69B44C` at `0x004f65d2` and invokes the singleton deleting destructor when non-null.
- Touched state: five direct lifecycle writes/clears and seven direct reads. The reads are concentrated in map/living-object effect descriptor lookup, `EffectObjectPane` setup/rendering, and application shutdown paths.
- This slot stays distinct from the neighboring static-object singleton at `0x0069b448`, light-object singleton at `0x0069b450`, and the runtime effects module that consumes effect image resources.
