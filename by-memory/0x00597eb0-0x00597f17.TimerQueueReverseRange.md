## Behavior

Reverses a logical range of scheduled timer records in the queue's ring slot array. Each swap copies a 20-byte [UID:0001VX][ScheduledTimerEvent](by-type/by-struct/ScheduledTimerEvent.md) record as a 16-byte block plus the trailing dword. It is a local primitive used by the rotate helper, not a public scheduler API.
