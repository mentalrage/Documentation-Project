## Summary

This `.rdata` subrange contains the RTTI/vtable pointer layout for clan item dialogs, clan bank panes, and bank item-list panes. It starts at the RTTI/vtable prelude for `ClanItemDialog` and ends immediately before the clan resource string/pointer block at `0x006163dc`.
