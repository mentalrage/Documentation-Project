## Behavior

`BaramApp::OnDeactivate` is the background/minimize transition handler for the game-client specialization of `Application`. It exits immediately when the transition guard byte at `this + 0x83a` is set. Otherwise it clears the active byte at `this + 0x839`, minimizes the main window and pauses the map-pane path when `dword_67A93C` is clear and [UID:0000S7][g_pScreenPane](by-global/g_pScreenPane.md) / `dword_67A7CC` is present, pauses event/background work, cancels or restores the active map/parcel-walk path through `dword_67A764`, and pauses sound/music state through `dword_67A7D0`, `dword_67A7C8`, and `dword_67AB4C`.
