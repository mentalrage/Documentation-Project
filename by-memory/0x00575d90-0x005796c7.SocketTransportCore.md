## Ownership Decision

The Socket methods in this cluster belong to `network/Socket.cpp`. It should be the source anchor for packet framing, receive parsing, transport setup, and connection state. The free transform helpers inside the address neighborhood belong with [UID:0000M9][PacketTransform](by-file/PacketTransform.md) or a private packet-transform section of `Socket.cpp`, not with [UID:0000M8][PacketBuffer](by-file/PacketBuffer.md). This aggregate is therefore reconstructable as evidence, but not attachable as one clean parent child until exact Socket, PacketTransform, and STL support children are separated.

The [UID:000245][0x00578f70-0x005797ae.StlTreeStringMapSupport](by-memory/0x00578f70-0x005797ae.StlTreeStringMapSupport.md) family is compiler/STL support and should be reconstructed through owner container declarations, not as Socket source.
