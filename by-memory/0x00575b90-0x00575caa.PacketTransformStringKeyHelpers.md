## Covered Range

This page covers two transform-adjacent helpers identified while splitting the former `0x00575abb-0x00575cb0` UNKNOWN row. They are not scalar [UID:0000M8][PacketBuffer](by-file/PacketBuffer.md) readers; both operate on packet transform key material and the `0x0069ba4c` / `0x0069ba58` / `0x0069ba94` global region.

| `0x00575c30-0x00575caa` | `sub_575C30` | Live helper called after login-name conversion; copies the login/process string into `ArgList`/`g_processArgList` at `0x0069ba58`, then repeatedly concatenates the string with itself for 31 more iterations. |

## Ownership Decision

Keep these helpers with [UID:0000M9][PacketTransform](by-file/PacketTransform.md) or a private transform section inside [UID:0000NS][Socket](by-file/Socket.md). They derive transform key material from process/login text and update the same globals used by nonce initialization and XOR transforms. They should not be folded into [UID:0000M8][PacketBuffer](by-file/PacketBuffer.md), which owns scalar packet serialization/parsing.

The current parent is `PacketTransform` because the helper pair mutates transform globals and is cross-referenced by the nonce/key-table documentation. Socket remains a consumer/lifetime neighbor, not the cleanest owner for these free helper routines.
