## Storage

`0x0067ab50-0x0067ab54` is the four-byte `.data` storage used by the active [UID:00008I][MiscWorkThread](by-class/MiscWorkThread.md) singleton pointer.

The storage is used by application cleanup and by feature paths that submit asynchronous HTTP, NCA/auth, and browse-directory work. It should remain owned by the background worker module, not by [UID:0000I0][CashShopRequest](by-file/CashShopRequest.md).
