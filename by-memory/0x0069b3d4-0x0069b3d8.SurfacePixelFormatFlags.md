## Interpretation

The surface initialization path at [UID:0001G6][0x00557140-0x00559aef.SurfacePresentation](by-memory/0x00557140-0x00559aef.SurfacePresentation.md) sets these flags while selecting software render callbacks. The byte at `0x0069b3d5` is the broad consumer-facing flag: decoded EPF tiles, screenshot/proof capture, and pixel conversion helpers check it before choosing compatibility conversion logic.

Keep the final names provisional until the render mode pass has separated DirectDraw surface-format detection, RGB555/RGB565 selection, and screenshot conversion behavior.

- Live IDA MCP reports seven direct refs to `0x0069b3d4`: writes at `0x005586cf`, `0x005586eb`, `0x005587c4`, `0x00558b56`, `0x00558b72`, and `0x00558e79`, plus the presentation/read path at `0x0055960e`.
