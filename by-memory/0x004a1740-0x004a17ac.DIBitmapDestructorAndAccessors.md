## IDA Evidence

- `0x004a1746` stores the [UID:0001XE][DIBitmapDirectXVtables](by-type/by-vtable/DIBitmapDirectXVtables.md) address `0x00618e54`.
- The raw destructor body loads `m_hBitmap` from `+0x2c`, calls `DeleteObject` when non-null, restores `ecx`, and tail-jumps to `sub_4F4A90`, the `LObject` destructor path.
- IDA models the four accessors at `0x004a1760`, `0x004a1770`, `0x004a1780`, and `0x004a1790` as functions, but does not model function starts at `0x004a1740` or `0x004a17a0`.
- `xrefs_to 0x004a1770` reports callers at `0x00581dd9` and `0x00581df8`.
- `xrefs_to 0x004a1780` reports callers at `0x0058127a`, `0x005812dc`, `0x00581946`, `0x005819a6`, and `0x00581de5`.
- `xrefs_to 0x004a1790` reports callers at `0x00581286`, `0x005812e9`, `0x00581953`, `0x005819b3`, and `0x00581dee`.
- IDA reports no direct xrefs to `0x004a1760` or `0x004a17a0` in the current database.

## Generated-Data Caveat

Current `source-3/simroot_v2/class_DIBitmap.cpp` emits the constructor and scalar deleting destructor but omits this whole `0x004a1740-0x004a17ac` method cluster. Current `class_DIBitmap.meta_wave3` also reports `vtable_count: 0` despite the IDA-confirmed vtable and stores. Treat the generated file as incomplete for this class until Wave3 data is regenerated/fixed.

Current `class_DIBitmap.meta_wave3` also reports a `0x60`-byte structure with broad undefined fields. The field offsets used here are from IDA-confirmed instructions and are documented in [UID:0001U6][DIBitmapLayout](by-type/by-struct/DIBitmapLayout.md).
