## Behavior

This is the ordinary non-deleting `AutoInit` teardown body. It stores the anonymous `AutoInit` vtable pointer into `this` and tail-jumps to `OleUninitialize`.

The compiler also emitted [UID:0000ZG][0x00470300-0x00470329.AutoInit](by-memory/0x00470300-0x00470329.AutoInit.md) at `0x00470300` and the static [UID:0001O6][0x0060c0f0-0x0060c100.AutoInitStaticShutdownThunk](by-memory/0x0060c0f0-0x0060c100.AutoInitStaticShutdownThunk.md) at `0x0060c0f0`. Do not count all three as independent handwritten OLE shutdown policies; they are alternate ABI/static-lifetime entry points around the same tiny destructor semantics.
