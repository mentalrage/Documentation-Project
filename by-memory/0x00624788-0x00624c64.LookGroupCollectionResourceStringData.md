## Summary

This exact `.rdata` subrange contains the look/group/collection resource string and constant island after the vtable data. The range begins at the `aLookextEpf` label immediately after the `LineIterator` one-slot vtable and ends before `0x00624c64`, where `SimpleListPane` RTTI/vtable data begins.
