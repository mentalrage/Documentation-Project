## Behavior

This is the ordinary non-deleting destructor for `StaticObjImageLib`. The authored body cleans the static-object record table, the `TILEC` archive catalog, and the tile-class table. The compiler then destroys direct bases in reverse declaration order:

1. Restore the [UID:0001YX][StaticObjImageLibVtable](by-type/by-vtable/StaticObjImageLibVtable.md) at `this + 0x00`.
2. Call `GetMemoryMan()` at `0x00516030` and retain the returned `MemoryMan *` for record and pointer-table cleanup.
3. If the static-object pointer table at `this + 0x0c` is non-null, iterate `this + 0x10` entries.
4. For each static-object record pointer, inspect its `+0x08` cache-chain pointer. If a chain is present, clear the record's `+0x08` link and free each chained node through `MemoryMan::FreeBufferMemory` at `0x00516170`, following each node's `+0x08` next pointer. If the chain is absent, free the record pointer itself through that method.
5. Free the static-object pointer table through `MemoryMan::FreeBufferMemory`.
6. Copy the tile-catalog/archive table pointer from `this + 0x04`, call `GetMemoryMan()` a second time, dereference `catalog->records` without an outer catalog-null guard, conditionally free that records pointer, then unconditionally free the catalog object through `MemoryMan::FreeBufferMemory`.
7. Free the tile-class table at `this + 0x08` through `j_j_j___free_base` at `0x005c7799`.
8. Implicitly destroy the direct empty `Singleton<StaticObjImageLib>` base, clearing [UID:0000SD][g_pStaticObjImageLib](by-global/g_pStaticObjImageLib.md) at `0x0069b448`.
9. Implicitly destroy the `LObject` base through cleanup at `0x004f4a90`.

Unlike the scalar deleting destructor at `0x004e6990`, this body does not test delete flags or call `operator delete(this)`.
