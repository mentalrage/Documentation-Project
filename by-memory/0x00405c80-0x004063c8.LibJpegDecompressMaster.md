# IJG libjpeg Decompress Master `0x00405c80-0x004063c8`

## Status

- Confidence: strong for `jpeg-6b/jdmaster.c` source-file ownership.
- Proposed owner: [UID:0000KN][LibJPEG](by-file/LibJPEG.md)
- Disposition: third-party static IJG libjpeg code; exclude from NexusTK product files.
- Evidence basis: read-only IDA MCP decompile/callee/function-list checks and source comparison against `jpegsrc.v6b.tar.gz` on 2026-05-25. `wave3.py` was not executed for this pass.

## Range Role

This range is IJG libjpeg's decompression master controller. It computes output dimensions, chooses merged upsampling/color conversion when possible, initializes downstream decoder modules, and coordinates output-pass setup.

The range starts at `0x00405c80`, after the documented memory-manager cluster, and ends at `0x004063c8`. `0x004063d0` begins generic IJG utility helpers in `jutils.c`.

## Evidence Notes

- `jpeg_start_decompress` at `0x004016e0` calls `0x00405fa0`, matching `jinit_master_decompress`.
- IDA decompilation of `0x00405fa0` allocates a 28-byte master object and installs callbacks to `0x00406240` and `0x004063a0`.
- IDA decompilation of `0x004063a0` checks `quantize_colors`, calls the color-quantizer finish-pass callback when needed, then increments the master controller's completed-pass counter, matching stock `finish_output_pass`.
- IDA decompilation of `0x00405fe0` calls the known decompression module initializers at `0x0040a440`, `0x004090b0`, `0x0040cc20`, `0x004075c0`, `0x00409e10`, `0x0040b230`, `0x00408a60`, `0x0040b820`, `0x0040abc0`, and `0x0040a0d0`, matching the `master_selection` fan-out in `jdmaster.c`.
- The range-limit table construction in `0x004061c0` matches the stock `prepare_range_limit_table` structure.

## Ownership Decision

Keep this range in `third_party/libjpeg/jdmaster.c`. It is pipeline-selection code for stock IJG decompression, not NexusTK image-rendering policy.
