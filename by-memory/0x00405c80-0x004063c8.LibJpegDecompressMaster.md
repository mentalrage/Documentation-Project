## Range Role

This range is IJG libjpeg's decompression master controller. It computes output dimensions, chooses merged upsampling/color conversion when possible, initializes downstream decoder modules, and coordinates output-pass setup.

The range starts at `0x00405c80`, after the documented memory-manager cluster, and ends at `0x004063c8`. `0x004063d0` begins generic IJG utility helpers in `jutils.c`.
