## Behavior

`DrawNumberGlyphString` renders a wide-character number string with the loaded `9X11FONT.BIN` table. It skips spaces and slashes, advances by 9 pixels for every scanned character, computes a glyph index from the character value and glyph row, and renders a 9 by 11 source rectangle into the destination rectangle.
