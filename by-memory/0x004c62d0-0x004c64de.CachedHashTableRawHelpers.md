## Layout Touchpoints

- `+0x04` is the fixed-entry storage buffer.
- `+0x08` is the parallel slot-state byte buffer.
- `+0x0c` is the fixed entry size.
- `+0x10` is the slot count and hash divisor.
- `+0x14` is the occupied entry count.
