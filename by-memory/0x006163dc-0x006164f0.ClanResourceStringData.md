## Summary

This `.rdata` subrange contains wide resource-name strings and format strings used by the clan status, modal dialog, and clan-bank UI code. It follows the clan-bank vtable block and ends immediately before the `CollectionDialogPane` RTTI locator at `0x006164f0`.

IDA names several starts in this range as `off_6163DC`, `off_6163F0`, `off_616404`, `off_616418`, and `off_616484`, but the bytes at those addresses are not pointer cells. Live byte review shows they are the UTF-16 starts of full resource names such as `CLAN1.EPF`, `CLAN2.EPF`, `CLAN3.EPF`, `CLANBUT.EPF`, and `CLANBAN1.PAL`. The adjacent labels such as `aAn1Epf`, `aLan1Epf`, and `aAnban1Epf` are interior suffix labels inside those full strings.

Autogen handling: `AUTOGEN_PARENT_UID` intentionally remains blank.
