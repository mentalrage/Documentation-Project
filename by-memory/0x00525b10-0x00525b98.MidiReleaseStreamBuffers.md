## Behavior

This helper releases the two WinMM MIDI stream buffer/header slots used by the `MidiPlayer` stream pipeline. If `g_midiHeadersPrepared` is nonzero, it walks the two header records at `0x0069b528` and `0x0069b57c`, calls `midiOutUnprepareHeader` for the leading `MIDIHDR` in each 84-byte slot, and clears `g_midiHeadersPrepared`.

It then walks the same two slots and, for any non-null `MIDIHDR::lpData`, obtains the backing `HGLOBAL`, unlocks it, obtains the handle again, frees it, and clears `lpData`. The repeated `GlobalHandle` call before unlock and before free is intentional binary behavior and is preserved in the formal C++.
