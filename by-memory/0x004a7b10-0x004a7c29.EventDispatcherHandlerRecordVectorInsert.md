## Behavior

This helper inserts a 28-byte event-handler record into the dispatcher handler-record vector. It is the grow/insert half of the record-vector support paired with [UID:000148][0x004a87a0-0x004a8810.EventDispatcherHandlerRecordVectorFree](by-memory/0x004a87a0-0x004a8810.EventDispatcherHandlerRecordVectorFree.md) and the adjacent relink/reallocation helpers at [UID:00022A][0x004a82b0-0x004a8795.EventDispatcherHandlerRecordRelinkHelpers](by-memory/0x004a82b0-0x004a8795.EventDispatcherHandlerRecordRelinkHelpers.md).

Observed behavior from IDA MCP:

- Computes the insertion index from `Src - begin`, divided by 28.
- Checks the maximum element count and calls the overflow handler when the vector would exceed its limit.
- Computes a growth capacity from current capacity and requested element count.
- Allocates a replacement record buffer through the adjacent allocator helper at [UID:00022B][0x004a8820-0x004a88d2.EventDispatcherHandlerRecordVectorAllocationHelpers](by-memory/0x004a8820-0x004a88d2.EventDispatcherHandlerRecordVectorAllocationHelpers.md).
- Copies the new 28-byte record into the insertion slot.
- Moves the prefix and suffix record ranges with `memmove`.
- Publishes the replacement begin/end/capacity pointers through the adjacent rebind helper at `0x004a86b0`.
- Returns the address of the inserted record in the new buffer.
- 2026-05-31 recheck: IDA MCP `lookup_funcs` reports `sub_4A7B10` size `0x11a`; decompilation confirms all element-count math divides byte spans by `28` and copies the inserted record as 16 + 8 + 4 bytes.
