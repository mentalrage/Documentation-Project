## Behavior

Constructs a blocking fixed-size ring buffer. The exact body is `0x104` / 260 bytes. It initializes the implicit `LObject` base and RingBuffer vptr as compiler lowering, calls `GetMemoryMan()` before any owned-object allocation, and keeps that singleton pointer live in EDI. It then allocates `Monitor`, `MonitorCondition(mMonitor)` for not-full, and `MonitorCondition(mMonitor)` for not-empty in that order; stores `elementSize` at `+0x10` and `capacity` at `+0x14`; calls `MemoryMan::ZeroAllocateBufferMemory(mElementSize * mCapacity)` into `mStorage +0x18`; and finally clears `mCount +0x1c`, `mReadIndex +0x20`, and `mWriteIndex +0x24`.

The cached local `MemoryMan *memoryMan` is source-significant because `GetMemoryMan()` executes before all three object allocations and its result survives until the later storage allocation.
