## Behavior

Inserts a 20-byte [UID:0001VX][ScheduledTimerEvent](by-type/by-struct/ScheduledTimerEvent.md) at a chosen logical position in the timer queue. The helper grows the slot pointer array when full, chooses whether to open space at the head or tail based on which side of the insertion point is cheaper, allocates a scheduled-event record when the destination slot is empty, copies the record into that slot, increments count, and uses the rotate helper to move surrounding entries into sorted position.

This is called by `TimerMgr::ScheduleTimer` after that method finds the sorted insertion point by comparing due ticks.

B015 source-quality reanalysis resolves the source-facing signature direction as `TimerMgrTimerQueue::InsertTimer(Iterator where, const ScheduledTimerEvent &event)`. It confirms true half-open body `0x00598480-0x005986da`; the filename/title end `0x005986d9` is the final executable byte convention.
