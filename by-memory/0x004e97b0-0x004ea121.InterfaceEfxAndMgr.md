## Boundary Evidence

- `lookup_funcs` confirms the listed starts, shows `sub_4EA090` ending at `0x004ea121`, and shows `0x004ea130` as the next unrelated function.
- Decompilation of `0x004ea130` shows `InventoryPane` vtable setup and an `InterfaceEfx` child allocation; it is a caller/consumer, not `InterfaceEfxMgr`.
- `callers 0x004e9d00` reports the manager constructor call from [UID:0000UV][InitializeMainUiGraph_004F7D10](by-item/InitializeMainUiGraph_004F7D10.md) at `0x004f8ad8`.
- `xrefs_to 0x004e9ee0` reports one direct call from [UID:0000JQ][GeneralPurposePanel](by-file/GeneralPurposePanel.md) at `0x004b880b`.
- `decompile 0x004e9ee0` confirms the client option gate and dispatch to the manager-owned character, item, and magic interface effects.
- `xrefs_to 0x0069b360` confirms the active manager singleton is written/cleared inside this range.
- `xrefs_to 0x004ea06b`, `xrefs_to 0x004ea076`, and `xrefs_to 0x004ea090` confirm the secondary destructor adjustors are vtable-referenced and forward into the scalar deleting destructor.

## Data Caveats

- Active `class_InterfaceEfxMgr.cpp` omits the IDA-confirmed `0x004e9ee0` trigger helper and `0x004ea060` singleton clear helper.
- Active `class_InterfaceEfxMgr.cpp` omits adjustor thunks `0x004ea06b` and `0x004ea076` from emitted source, although metadata records them as excluded.
- Active `class_InterfaceEfx.cpp` includes generated helper/base labels that point at fitting-room/text-button code; treat those labels as shared-helper artifacts until the base class is fully resolved.
- `0x00597600` was previously documented as `InterfaceEfx::RemoveFromUpdateScheduler`, but IDA body/caller evidence now supports generic [UID:0001K8][0x005975e0-0x0059760d.TimerHandlerScheduleRemoveWrappers](by-memory/0x005975e0-0x0059760d.TimerHandlerScheduleRemoveWrappers.md) ownership. InterfaceEfx is a caller, not the source owner for that range.
