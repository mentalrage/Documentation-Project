## Storage

`0x0069af20` is a one-byte `.data` flag initialized to `0xff`. EventDispatcher routing reads it before firing the Shift+Ctrl+`3` or Shift+Ctrl+`4` shortcut actions, sets it to `1` after either action fires, and resets it to `0` when the modified-key branch falls through without accepting one of those chords.
