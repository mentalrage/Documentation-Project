# DATFile Destructor

## Role And Evidence

Exact ordinary destructor at `[0x0049c160,0x0049c179)`. It clears the active record and archive pointers, then permits normal base destruction. It does not own or delete the mapped archive container. The scalar deleting wrapper at `0x0049d280` is a separate compiler product.

## Ownership And Boundaries

Owned by [UID:00003G][DATFile](by-class/DATFile.md), emitted through [UID:0000IN][DATFile](by-file/DATFile.md), and distinct from manager/container destruction.

## Historical Assumptions

Explicit vtable restoration and scalar-delete flag handling are compiler output and are not authored here.
