## Instruction Notes

- The constructor stores a `CriticalSection *` at `this + 0x00`, enters the same object, returns `this` at the ABI level, and uses `retn 4`; the binary inlines the one-call `Enter` body to the Win32 import.
- The destructor leaves the stored object and returns with plain `retn`; its binary likewise inlines the one-call `Leave` body.
- Application callers pass [UID:000529][0x0067a9a4-0x0067a9bc.ApplicationMessageHandlerCriticalSection](by-memory/0x0067a9a4-0x0067a9bc.ApplicationMessageHandlerCriticalSection.md), an exact 24-byte `CriticalSection` static.
- Zero direct xrefs to the standalone Enter/Leave bodies are preserved as optimizer evidence, not as a reason to expose raw `LPCRITICAL_SECTION` in the human declaration.
- The next code body after the ignored padding is `0x0049bd20-0x0049bd2c`, followed by `0x0049bd2c-0x0049bd30` padding and `DATFileMgr::DATFileMgr` at `0x0049bd30`.
