## Library Boundary

`DecodeJpfImageToTileContext` and `DecodeJpegBufferToTileContext` are NexusTK-owned wrappers over the statically linked [UID:0000VP][0x00401000-0x00414283.LibJpeg6bCore](by-memory/0x00401000-0x00414283.LibJpeg6bCore.md). They call `jpeg_std_error`-style setup at `0x00401000`, `jpeg_CreateDecompress` at `0x004011b0` with version `62`, in-memory source setup through [UID:0004DP][0x004e7030-0x004e7094.InitializeJpegSourceManagerRecord](by-memory/0x004e7030-0x004e7094.InitializeJpegSourceManagerRecord.md), read-header/start/read-scanline/finish/destroy helpers documented in [UID:0000VQ][0x004011b0-0x004019e3.LibJpegDecompressApiFrontEnd](by-memory/0x004011b0-0x004019e3.LibJpegDecompressApiFrontEnd.md), then convert 24-bit RGB scanlines to RGB565. B010's accepted marker-reader pass identifies the callback slot written by `0x004e7030` as [UID:0000VR][0x00401b00-0x0040391f.LibJpegMarkerReader](by-memory/0x00401b00-0x0040391f.LibJpegMarkerReader.md) `jpeg_resync_to_restart`; this is a product-to-IJG source-manager hook, not product marker parsing.

Do not move IJG internals into `ImageLoaders.cpp`. The project-owned layer is the file-format wrapper and tile-context conversion policy.

`DecodeZpfFpfToTileContext` is similarly project-owned wrapper glue over a compressed format: it recognizes the outer `ZPF`, inflates into a stack `_AUTOBUF<unsigned char>` temporary through zlib `Uncompress`, validates inner `FPF`, and copies the 16-bit body into the same tile-context shape. The wrapper's second formal size argument is present for the source-family convention but unused in this optimized body.

`DecodeFpfToTileContext` is the direct/raw sibling of the ZPF wrapper. It consumes an `FPF` payload without the outer decompression step, copies the same 16-bit body format, and tail-calls the same post-decode finalizer. The child page now documents the raw PE offset/hash, exact `FpfHeader` fields read by the helper, descriptive source signature, and first-draft C++ while keeping original-name and no-caller caveats below final-audit confidence.

`DecodePngFileToTileContext` and `DecodePngResourceToTileContext` are NexusTK-owned wrappers over the statically linked [UID:0000KW][LodePNG](by-file/LodePNG.md) front ends at `0x00443c40` and `0x00443b40`. Do not move LodePNG internals into `ImageLoaders.cpp`; the project-owned layer here is file/resource staging plus conversion from RGBA8 decode output into the `EPFTileContext` RGB565 primary pixels and optional `alphaData` plane. B004's EPFTileContext pass distinguishes that plane from encoded-mask bytes at `+0x20/+0x24`.
