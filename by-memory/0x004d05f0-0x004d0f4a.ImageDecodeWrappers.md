## Library Boundary

`DecodeJpfImageToTileContext` and `DecodeJpegBufferToTileContext` are NexusTK-owned wrappers over the statically linked [UID:0000VP][0x00401000-0x00414283.LibJpeg6bCore](by-memory/0x00401000-0x00414283.LibJpeg6bCore.md). They call `jpeg_std_error`-style setup at `0x00401000`, `jpeg_CreateDecompress` at `0x004011b0` with version `62`, in-memory source setup at `0x004e7030`, read-header/start/read-scanline/finish/destroy helpers documented in [UID:0000VQ][0x004011b0-0x004019e3.LibJpegDecompressApiFrontEnd](by-memory/0x004011b0-0x004019e3.LibJpegDecompressApiFrontEnd.md), then convert 24-bit RGB scanlines to RGB565.

Do not move IJG internals into `ImageLoaders.cpp`. The project-owned layer is the file-format wrapper and tile-context conversion policy.

`DecodeZpfFpfToTileContext` is similarly project-owned wrapper glue over a compressed format: it recognizes the outer `ZPF`, inflates into a temporary buffer, validates inner `FPF`, and copies the 16-bit body into the same tile-context shape.

`DecodeFpfToTileContext` is the direct/raw sibling of the ZPF wrapper. It consumes an `FPF` payload without the outer decompression step, copies the same 16-bit body format, and tail-calls the same post-decode finalizer.

`DecodePngFileToTileContext` and `DecodePngResourceToTileContext` are NexusTK-owned wrappers over the statically linked [UID:0000KW][LodePNG](by-file/LodePNG.md) front ends at `0x00443c40` and `0x00443b40`. Do not move LodePNG internals into `ImageLoaders.cpp`; the project-owned layer here is file/resource staging plus conversion from RGBA8 decode output into the `EPFTileContext` RGB565 and mask/alpha planes.

## Ownership Decision

Keep these helpers with [UID:0000K3][ImageLoaders](by-file/ImageLoaders.md) or a nearby legacy `ImageDecode.cpp` module. The callers span profile portraits, remote look panes, minimap data, and login background art, so the helper group is shared image/resource infrastructure rather than owned by any one feature.

`RECONSTRUCTABLE` is true because these are project-facing wrapper functions rather than IJG or LodePNG internals. This aggregate and all seven child pages attach to [UID:0000K3][ImageLoaders](by-file/ImageLoaders.md). The raw FPF and PNG-file helpers are attached at threshold confidence because their source-family placement and behavior are verified, but their missing function object/caller evidence still blocks C++ emission.

Leave `RECONSTRUCTION_CPP` blank until the `EPFTileContext`, palette helper, and compressed-wrapper names are final enough to produce source-quality C++.
