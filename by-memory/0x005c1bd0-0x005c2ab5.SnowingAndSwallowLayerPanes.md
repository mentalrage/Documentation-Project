- Disposition: reconstructable NexusTK project code

## Boundary Notes

The older [UID:0001O0][0x005c1bd0-0x005c2a5a.SnowingLayerPane](by-memory/0x005c1bd0-0x005c2a5a.SnowingLayerPane.md) page is a legacy aggregate over Snowing-owned child methods, not a contiguous Snowing-owned source range. `SwallowLayerPane` begins at `0x005c2230`, and the destructor tail at `0x005c29ec-0x005c2ab5` alternates Snowing and Swallow thunks/destructors.

Use this page for migration planning. Do not assign `0x005c2230-0x005c299e`, `0x005c2a02-0x005c2a17`, or `0x005c2a60-0x005c2ab5` to SnowingLayerPane just because they fall inside the older aggregate filename.

- Current `simroot_v2` emits the modeled Snowing and Swallow functions under their class files, but `class_BackPane.cpp` still emits the raw `0x005c2760` and `0x005c2840` Swallow-shaped helper bodies with wrong BackPane scroll-animation names.
