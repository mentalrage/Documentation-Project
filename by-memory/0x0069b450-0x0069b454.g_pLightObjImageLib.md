## Evidence Notes

- Live IDA MCP on 2026-06-05 reports item head `0x0069b450`, size `4`, segment `.data`, name `dword_69B450`, bytes `ff ff ff ff`, and 19 direct xrefs.
- `0x004df7e0-0x004dfaa6` is the singleton constructor body. It stores `this` at `0x004df82b`, clears the slot on the constructor guard/fallback path at `0x004df832`, installs the `LightObjImageLib` and adjacent `ProtectedArray<LightInfo>` vtables, opens `LIGHT.TBL`, allocates `0x1c`-byte `LightInfo` rows, and creates radial light-frame objects.
- IDA callers show `0x004df7e0` has one direct startup caller at `0x004f6139` inside `0x004f5fb0-0x004f6490`.
- `0x004dfab0-0x004dfb36` is the raw ordinary destructor body. IDA does not model it as a function start, but the write xref at `0x004dfb23` clears `dword_69B450` after light-frame and record-buffer cleanup.
- `0x004e5bb0-0x004e5bbb` is a tiny cleanup helper whose whole body is `dword_69B450 = 0`.
- `0x004e65e0-0x004e669d` is the scalar deleting destructor body and clears `dword_69B450` at `0x004e665d` before conditional object deletion.
- `0x004f6490-0x004f66a5` is called by `_WinMain@16` at `0x004f5e8e`; its shutdown sweep reads `dword_69B450` at `0x004f6642` and invokes the singleton deleting destructor when non-null.
- Touched state: five direct lifecycle writes/clears and fourteen direct reads. The reads are concentrated in attached-object/light render paths plus the application shutdown sweep.
- This slot stays distinct from the neighboring effect-object singleton at `0x0069b44c`, the later IME/world-map pane globals, and the runtime consumers that only draw light overlays.
