- `pixelCount >> 1` is the packed-dword pair count. Most cases process 16 pairs per 64-byte SIMD chunk, then remaining pairs, then one 16-bit tail when `pixelCount & 1` is nonzero. Case 9 uses a four-pair/16-byte vector chunk but has the same packed and scalar expression.
- Source expresses the packed-pair loop and odd tail once through `APPLY_RGB565_FADE`; compiler vector scheduling remains an optimization and is not represented with IDA intrinsics or labels.
