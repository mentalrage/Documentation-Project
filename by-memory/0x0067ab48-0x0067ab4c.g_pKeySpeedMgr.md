- The fast-repeat helper callers are `0x00463ab5` and `0x00466a24`.
- B001-014 IDA MCP split confirms [UID:00032A][0x004673c0-0x004673d1.DestroyKeySpeedMgr](by-memory/0x004673c0-0x004673d1.DestroyKeySpeedMgr.md) is the missing cleanup xref: it loads `dword_67AB48`, null-checks it, and invokes the deleting destructor through the vtable. Its only direct caller found is [UID:00019G][0x004f5c80-0x004f5f17.WinMain](by-memory/0x004f5c80-0x004f5f17.WinMain.md) at `0x004f5eeb`, so the wrapper is WinMain cleanup glue while this global remains KeySpeedMgr-owned storage.

Constructor decompilation at `0x004efea0` initializes the `LObject` base, stores `this` or zero into `dword_67AB48`, installs `KeySpeedMgr::vftable`, and initializes the saved delay/speed fields to `-1`.

The non-deleting destructor at `0x004efee0` restores the vtable, clears `dword_67AB48`, and chains to base cleanup. The scalar-deleting destructor at `0x004effc0` performs the same singleton clear and base cleanup, then conditionally frees the object.

The restore helper at `0x004eff30` applies saved keyboard delay with `SystemParametersInfoW(SPI_SETKEYBOARDDELAY, ...)` when the saved delay is in `0..3`, then applies saved speed with `SystemParametersInfoW(SPI_SETKEYBOARDSPEED, ...)` when the saved speed is in `0..31`. The fast-repeat helper at `0x004effa0` sets delay `0` and speed `31`.
