# JsonCpp OurReader Error and Token Helpers

## Status

- Address range: `0x00431f20-0x00432e10`
- Entity kind: statically linked third-party JsonCpp parser helper island
- Disposition: ignored third-party library code; keep with [UID:0000KI][JsonCpp](by-file/JsonCpp.md).
- Confidence: strong for JsonCpp ownership; high for the observed `OurReader` helper identities.
- Evidence basis: read-only IDA MCP function/byte/disassembly/decompile checks on 2026-05-27 plus source comparison against the staged JsonCpp `b299d3581f4dc670734f1fe1a34fce1282337802` snapshot. `wave3.py` was not executed.

## Range Role

This range fills the former coverage gap between [UID:0000X7][0x00431d50-0x00431f1c.JsonCppOurReaderParse](by-memory/0x00431d50-0x00431f1c.JsonCppOurReaderParse.md) and [UID:0000X8][0x00432e10-0x004345ee.JsonCppSecondParserIsland](by-memory/0x00432e10-0x004345ee.JsonCppSecondParserIsland.md). It is the second `Json::OurReader` helper island after the `parse` front end and before the larger value/object/array parser functions. The code covers formatted error export, structured error export, `pushError` overloads, token scanning, whitespace/literal matching, comment scanning, and raw string/comment scan helpers.

The range is third-party JsonCpp code, not NexusTK-owned product logic. Rebuild source should come from the staged `third_party/jsoncpp/src/lib_json/json_reader.cpp` file, not from hand-reconstructed product modules.

## Evidence

- IDA reports `sub_431D50` as ending at `0x00431f1c`; bytes `0x00431f1c-0x00431f20` are `0xcc` alignment padding, and the next real helper starts at `0x00431f20`.
- `sub_431F20` references the exact error-format literals used by upstream `OurReader::getFormattedErrorMessages()`: `* `, newline, two spaces, `See `, and ` for detail.\n`.
- `sub_432460` iterates the stored error container and emits start/limit offsets relative to the reader input base, matching upstream `OurReader::getStructuredErrors()`.
- `sub_4325D0` and `sub_4326C0` validate `Value` offsets against `end_ - begin_` and append `ErrorInfo` records, matching the two upstream `pushError` overloads.
- `sub_4327D0` skips whitespace, sets token start/end locations, and switches on the next character. It calls the local number helper at `0x00432e10`, the literal matcher at `0x00432b10`, and the comment reader at `0x00432b60`.
- The `readToken` string matches include `nfinity`, `alse`, `ull`, and `aN`, matching the staged JsonCpp special-float, `false`, `null`, and `NaN` token cases.
- The raw helpers at `0x00432d00`, `0x00432d50`, `0x00432d90`, and `0x00432dd0` match the staged `readCStyleComment`, `readCppStyleComment`, double-quote string scan, and single-quote string scan loops.

Keep this range with [UID:0000KI][JsonCpp](by-file/JsonCpp.md) under `third_party/jsoncpp/`. It should be obtained from the staged JsonCpp source snapshot and excluded from NexusTK project-code reconstruction.

## Open Questions

- The source-level identities are high confidence, but IDA has not named the raw helper starts at `0x00432ae0`, `0x00432d00`, `0x00432d50`, `0x00432d90`, and `0x00432dd0` as functions. Keep their bounds documented here unless a later IDA analysis pass creates exact function objects.
