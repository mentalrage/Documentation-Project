## Behavior

This helper recomputes the item-inventory scrollbar position from pointer coordinates and scrollbar geometry. It reads the current position at offset `+0xfe`, computes the updated short position from the track span and drag/click coordinates, then notifies the owning [UID:0000LS][NewInventoryPane](by-file/NewInventoryPane.md) when the position changes.

The owner callback call is `0x004eba00`, reached through a virtual owner lookup at vtable slot `+0x1c`, with arguments `(orientation, oldPosition, newPosition)`.

The live 2026-06-07 decompile shows the helper first gets part rectangle `2` through `0x005640a0`, then translates screen-relative pointer coordinates through `0x004a9090` and `0x005447c0`. It branches on orientation byte `+0xfc`: the non-zero path uses the vertical coordinate and drag anchor `+0x10c`, while the zero path uses the horizontal coordinate and drag anchor `+0x108`. Both paths clamp against the computed thumb/track rectangle, scale through the max range word at `+0x100`, compare against the old position at `+0xfe`, and call the owner callback only on change.
