## Behavior

- Classic mode draws from `SCRL001.EPD` and `SCRL001.PAD`.
- EPF mode clears the thumb/background area, draws stretch/fill slices from `SLIDEBG.EPF`/`SLIDEBG.PAL`, and overlays `SCRBUTT.EPF` with `BUTTON.PAL`.
- The function skips drawing when the clipped thumb rectangle is outside the pane clip.
- Disabled/empty scrollbar states draw a background or clear the relevant area depending on mode and state flags.
- IDA confirms four direct calls to `ScrollPane::GetScrollPartRect` at `0x0055ce8f`, `0x0055cf4c`, `0x0055d664`, and `0x0055d77f`.
