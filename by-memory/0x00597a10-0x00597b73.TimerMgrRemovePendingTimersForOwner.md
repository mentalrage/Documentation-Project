## Behavior

This method walks the queue backward from the last logical entry to the first, removing every [UID:0001VX][ScheduledTimerEvent](by-type/by-struct/ScheduledTimerEvent.md) whose owner pointer equals the supplied `TimerHandler` owner. For each match, it calls [UID:0002L6][0x00598290-0x00598471.TimerMgrTimerQueueSpliceTimerRange](by-memory/0x00598290-0x00598471.TimerMgrTimerQueueSpliceTimerRange.md) on the one-entry range. After the pass, it refreshes `m_nextDueTick` from the new first logical queue entry or sets it to `0xffffffff` if the queue is empty.

Walking backward is important: removing the current logical entry does not invalidate the unvisited lower indexes.
