## Behavior

This helper tokenizes a five-argument startup command line, accepts either a hostname or dotted IPv4 address, stores the resolved endpoint plus two trailing launcher strings into the startup-state block, and aborts on DNS or validation failure.

On success it writes the resolved IPv4 bytes to `param_1 + 0x2915b4`, the parsed port to `param_1 + 0x2916c4`, sets `param_1 + 0x2916ca` to `1`, sets state byte `param_1 + 0x28de28` to `5`, and converts two trailing wide arguments into multibyte strings at `0x28d9e0` and `0x28da20`.
