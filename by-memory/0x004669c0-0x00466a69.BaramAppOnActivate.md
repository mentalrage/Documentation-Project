## Behavior

`BaramApp::OnActivate` is the foreground transition handler for the game-client specialization of `Application`. It exits immediately when the transition guard byte at `this + 0x83a` is set. Otherwise it resumes timer/event ownership, restores the map pane if the client is not in the suppressed/minimized state, resumes background work, and resumes sound/music playback through the audio globals.
