## Exact Behavior And Call Flow

1. Initialize one local selection byte to zero.
2. Call [UID:000079][List](by-class/List.md) `InsertAt(index, 1, item)` on `m_items` at `this+0x130`.
3. Call `InsertAt(index, 1, &selected)` on `m_selectionFlags` at `this+0x138`, keeping both Lists index-aligned.
4. Call the inherited ListPane scrollbar synchronization helper.
5. Dispatch invalidation for `m_itemBounds` at `this+0x110`.

The original order is observable and retained: primary data first, parallel selection byte second, synchronization third, invalidation last. No selected-index adjustment or per-row selection callback occurs here.
