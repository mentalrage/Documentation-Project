## Behavior

This callback reads a parcel update packet whose first byte must be `8`. It decodes flag-dependent offsets, extracts left/right parcel counts from one packed byte, updates icon-pane counters, and calls `ParcelPane::SetParcelSlotData` on the associated child pane when the total or side counts changed.
