## Behavior

Timer/update callback for the flying parcel animation. While the frame byte is below `0x1e`, it moves/refreshes the pane and reschedules itself after 10 ms. Once the frame limit is reached, it removes the animation object, forwards the stored parcel counts into `g_pParcelPane`, sets the new/right activation flag, and invalidates the parcel pane.
