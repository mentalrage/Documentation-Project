This is the ordinary non-deleting `BrowserThread` destructor body immediately before `BrowserThread::RunMessageLoop`. It reinstalls the `BrowserThread` vtable, clears the global browser-thread singleton, and tail-calls the base `Thread` destructor.

`sub_596400` is documented as `Thread::~Thread` in [UID:0001JX][0x00596250-0x0059756e.ThreadAndThreadMan](by-memory/0x00596250-0x0059756e.ThreadAndThreadMan.md).

## Source-Level Interpretation

At source level this destructor's owner-specific policy is clearing [UID:0000QE][g_pBrowserThread](by-global/g_pBrowserThread.md). The vtable reinstall and tail-call to `Thread::~Thread` are compiler-emitted destructor mechanics for the derived/base destructor chain, so they should not be copied literally into final C++.

Formal reconstruction now emits the ordinary destructor as `BrowserThread::~BrowserThread()` with only the browser-specific singleton clear. The scalar deleting destructor at `0x004706f0-0x0047073b` is compiler ABI glue and receives a coverage comment there; the vtable reinstall and `Thread::~Thread` tail call remain compiler/base-destructor mechanics rather than handwritten source.
