This four-byte zero-initialized `.data` global stores the process-wide `EventMan` singleton. It is used by the Win32 message bridge, pane/list input helpers, cursor-position helpers, and event dispatch wrappers.

## Storage And Lifetime

| Address | Initial value | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `0x0067a754` | `0x00000000` | `EventMan*` singleton pointer |

Observed lifetime anchors:

- `0x004a8b40-0x004a8f13` constructs `EventMan`, stores `this` at `0x004a8b89`, and clears the slot on the constructor guard path at `0x004a8b90`.
- `0x004a8f20-0x004a8f35`, `0x004ab3e0-0x004ab3eb`, and `0x004ab440-0x004ab476` clear the singleton through destructor or small cleanup paths at `0x004a8f26`, `0x004ab3e0`, and `0x004ab44c`.
- `Main_WndProc` at `0x00465f20-0x0046673c` and UI helper paths read it for focus, cursor, mouse, keyboard, IME, and event dispatch work.
