It queries timer device capabilities, chooses `max(1, TIMECAPS.wPeriodMin)` capped to `TIMECAPS.wPeriodMax`, stores the chosen period in TimerMgr slot `+0x1c`, calls `timeBeginPeriod`, samples `timeGetTime`, stores the sampled tick in TimerMgr slot `+0x18`, and mirrors it into `dword_69BE10` and `dword_69BE0C`.
