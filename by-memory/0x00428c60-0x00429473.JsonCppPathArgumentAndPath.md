## Rule 26 Source-Mapping Detail

- The `PathArgument` constructors at `0x00428c60`, `0x00428c90`, `0x00428cc0`, and `0x00428d10` match the `json_value.cpp` constructor family around the `PathArgument` source definitions. The observed layout is a `JSONCPP_STRING` member at `+0`, `index_` at `+0x18`, and `kind_` at `+0x1c`.
- `0x00428d70` matches `Json::Path::Path(JSONCPP_STRING const& path, PathArgument const& a1, ..., PathArgument const& a5)`: it creates a temporary vector of five argument addresses, calls `makePath`, then releases the temporary vector.
- `0x00428ee0`, `0x00428f70`, and `0x00429030` match `Path::resolve(root)`, `Path::resolve(root, defaultValue)`, and `Path::make(root)`. They walk 32-byte `PathArgument` records, branch on `kind_ == 1` and `kind_ == 2`, and call JsonCpp array/object value helpers instead of product lookup helpers.
