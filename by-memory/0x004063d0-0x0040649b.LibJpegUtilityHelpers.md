## Range Role

This range is the retained IJG libjpeg 6b `jutils.c` helper-code cluster between decompression master control and compression marker writing. It contains five small utility routines for rounded arithmetic, row/block copying, and zeroing memory. Product image loaders and screenshot writers consume public libjpeg APIs; they do not own these helpers.
