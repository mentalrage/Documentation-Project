## Evidence Notes

- These small thunks and destructors cluster near other UI control destructor thunks, not near feature-dialog constructors.
- Generated destructor code sometimes names `TextButtonExControlPane::~Pane` or unrelated pane classes. Treat these as teardown-owner pollution until vtable/subobject ownership is fixed.
- IDA MCP `ida_funcs` reports 34 contiguous `0xb`-byte adjustor thunks from `0x0049af11` through `0x0049b087`; each thunk has vtable data references and forwards to a scalar deleting wrapper in this band.
- IDA MCP `ida_funcs` reports exact scalar deleting destructor boundaries including `0x0049b0d0-0x0049b10b`, `0x0049b110-0x0049b165`, `0x0049b170-0x0049b1ab`, `0x0049b1b0-0x0049b24f`, `0x0049b290-0x0049b2e5`, `0x0049b3b0-0x0049b417`, `0x0049b420-0x0049b487`, `0x0049b7a0-0x0049b854`, and `0x0049b860-0x0049b8c7`.
- Vtable scan maps the primary destructor cells to RTTI names: `ButtonControlPane` at `0x00617bd8`, `TextButtonControlPane` at `0x00617c7c`, `TextButtonExControlPane` at `0x00617d24`, `ImageButtonControlPane` at `0x00617dcc`, `RadioGroupControlPane` at `0x00617e70`, `SliderControlPane` at `0x00617f14`, `CheckBoxControlPane` at `0x006184d8`, and `RectangleControlPane` at `0x00618434`.
- The band crosses non-button neighbors. `EPFImageControlPane` cells at `0x00618248`, `0x006182b0`, and `0x006182e0`, and `TargetOptionEditControlPane` cells at `0x006182ec`, `0x00618354`, and `0x00618384`, are recorded here as exclusions/overlaps rather than ButtonControl-owned code.
- All observed inter-function gaps in `0x0049b087-0x0049b8d5` are `0xcc` padding.
- 2026-05-26 IDA MCP recheck confirms the `CheckBoxControlPane` adjustors at [UID:00011Z][0x0049af27-0x0049af3d.CheckBoxControlPaneAdjustorThunks](by-memory/0x0049af27-0x0049af3d.CheckBoxControlPaneAdjustorThunks.md) correspond to secondary vtable offsets `+0x0a0` and `+0x0a4`; both forward into scalar deleting destructor `0x0049b110`.
- 2026-05-26 IDA MCP recheck confirms the `NumericStringControlPane` and `RectangleControlPane` adjustor pairs are compiler-generated `this` adjustors into their scalar deleting destructors and are excluded through [UID:0000VN][-ignored](by-memory/-ignored.md).
