## Range Role

This range is the complete retained IJG libjpeg 6b `jdinput.c` decompression input controller. It validates frame/scan dimensions, sets up component sampling geometry, latches quantization tables, and switches `consume_input` between marker parsing and coefficient scan-data input.

The half-open range starts at `0x00404a60` with `jinit_input_controller` and ends at `0x0040505b`. Predecessor `jcomapi.c` ends at `0x00404a5c`, followed by four NOP bytes through `0x00404a60`. Five NOP bytes at `0x0040505b-0x00405060` separate this page from [UID:0000VW][0x00405060-0x00405c77.LibJpegMemoryManager](by-memory/0x00405060-0x00405c77.LibJpegMemoryManager.md) / `jmemmgr.c`.

All seven internal gaps are alignment rather than omitted functions or data: `0x00404aa1-0x00404ab0`, `0x00404ae9-0x00404af0`, `0x00404c95-0x00404ca0`, `0x00404d4c-0x00404d50`, `0x00404d61-0x00404d70`, `0x00404e2e-0x00404e30`, and `0x0040501c-0x00405020` contain NOPs. No split, data island, ignored subrange, or separate child is required.
