# zlib Public Compress/Checksum `0x004142c0-0x00414565`

## Status

- Confidence: strong for zlib 1.1.4 source identity and source-file split.
- Proposed owner: [UID:0000PC][Zlib](by-file/Zlib.md)
- Parent range: [UID:0000W3][0x004142c0-0x00419e56.ZlibCore](by-memory/0x004142c0-0x00419e56.ZlibCore.md)
- Evidence basis: official `zlib-1.1.4.tar.gz` source staged under [UID:0001QX][README](by-meta/obtained_thirdparty_files/README.md), read-only IDA MCP lookup/decompile/callee checks on 2026-05-25, and existing `simroot_v2` recovered wrappers. `wave3.py` was not executed for this pass.

## Range Role

This range contains the stock public zlib compression wrappers, decompression wrapper, and CRC-32 checksum helper at the head of the bundled zlib island.

## Function Map

| Range | zlib 1.1.4 source | Role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `0x004142c0-0x00414369` | `compress.c`: `compress2` | Initializes a local `z_stream`, calls `deflateInit_`, runs `deflate(..., Z_FINISH)`, writes the final output length, and calls `deflateEnd`. |
| `0x00414370-0x0041438f` | `compress.c`: `compress` | Thin default-level wrapper over `compress2` with level `Z_DEFAULT_COMPRESSION` / `-1`. |
| `0x00414390-0x0041442c` | `uncompr.c`: `uncompress` | Initializes a local inflate stream, calls `inflate(..., Z_FINISH)`, writes the output length, and calls `inflateEnd`. |
| `0x00414430-0x00414565` | `crc32.c`: `crc32` | Table-driven CRC-32 update routine over the zlib CRC table at `dword_63211C`. |

## Evidence

- IDA callees for `0x004142c0` are `0x00414570` (`deflateInit_`), `0x00414820` (`deflate`), and `0x00414b80` (`deflateEnd`), matching stock `compress2`.
- IDA callees for `0x00414370` contain only `0x004142c0`, matching stock `compress`.
- IDA callees for `0x00414390` are `0x00415a60` (`inflateInit_`), `0x00415a80` (`inflate`), and `0x00415900` (`inflateEnd`), matching stock `uncompress`.
- The staged zlib source places `compress2` in `compress.c`, `uncompress` in `uncompr.c`, and `crc32` in `crc32.c`. These names match the behavior and function order in the binary.
- `0x00414430` uses the standard zlib CRC table update shape and is called by product metadata validation code, but the helper itself remains third-party zlib code.

## Ownership Decision

Keep this range under `third_party/zlib/` with `compress.c`, `uncompr.c`, and `crc32.c`. Do not migrate `uncompress` or `crc32` into `MetaTable`, DAT, render-resource, or image-loader modules just because those product systems consume decompressed buffers.
