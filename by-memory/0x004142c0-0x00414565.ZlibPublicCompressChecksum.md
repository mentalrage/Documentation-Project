Keep this range under `NexusTK/third_party/zlib/` through [UID:0000PC][Zlib](by-file/Zlib.md), with source placement in stock `compress.c`, `uncompr.c`, and `crc32.c`. Do not migrate `compress`, `uncompress`, or `crc32` into product modules just because product systems consume compressed data.

- Image-loader, startup-logo, palette, DAT, resource, and render modules: rejected because they store or decode compressed assets but do not implement zlib.

- No-owner/non-emitting: rejected because canonical owner/emitter [UID:0000PC][Zlib](by-file/Zlib.md) is established and surfaces to `NexusTK/third_party/zlib/`.
