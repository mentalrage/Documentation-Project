## Behavior

The constructor accepts a caller-supplied rectangle-like bounds pointer. It computes:

- `width = rect.right - rect.left`
- `height = rect.bottom - rect.top`

It then delegates to `ListPane::ListPane` with fixed configuration values:

```text
ListPane(4, 10, width, height, 1, 1, 1)
```

After the base construction, it installs the `SimpleListPane` primary vtable at `0x00624c64`, secondary vtable at `0x00624cec`, and tertiary vtable at `0x00624d1c`.
