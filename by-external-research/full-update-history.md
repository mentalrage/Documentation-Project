# NexusTK / Baram Update And Version History

Research date: 2026-05-24

Scope: living external-research chronology assembled from official NexusTK/KRU pages, Nexus Atlas archives, Nexon/Korean Baram public pages, and third-party historical sources. This file is not yet exhaustive; it is the current sourced skeleton for later expansion.

Confidence: high for official/Nexon/Guinness entries, medium for Nexus Atlas archive entries that quote official posts, lower for fan-only interpretations. All entries need local confirmation before being used as binary ground truth.

## Method

- Use Nexus Atlas archive pages as the main US service chronology because they preserve older news, reset reports, and official reposts.
- Use official KRU pages for current client/download facts and old public guide behavior.
- Use Korean Baram official guide/update history as a separate upstream chronology, not as proof of US parity.
- Record client/version facts separately from content-only updates.

## Client Version Notes

Known public labels:

- `Nexus 5.31`: major January 2003 client. Full download reported at 50.5 MB; patch payload around 82 MB if using automatic patch for all changes.
- `Nexus 5.32`: immediate small patch over 5.31 for DirectX/list/icon/hair/map-load issues.
- `BARAM 6.0 client`: fan-maintained NexusWiki claim associated with 2005 KRU split and US rights.
- `Nexus TK 7.52`: current KRU official full installer, 227 MB.

Unknowns:

- Exact US versions between 1998 and 5.31.
- Exact transition from 5.32 to 6.0.
- Whether the local target executable has embedded version strings matching any public label.

## Update Themes By Subsystem

### Graphics And Assets

- 5.31 brought new faces and new weapon/armor/item images.
- 5.31/5.32 exposed legend/who-list icon bugs and hair/emotion issues.
- 2004 Vortex added over 100 items with new graphics and drew from previously unused graphics.
- Korean 2003 update history lists a graphics renewal, but a direct connection to US 5.31 is unproven.

### Map And Rendering

- 5.31 introduced a revised map system for smoother/faster rendering and improved map functionality.
- 5.31 launch observations mention a new map/stable.
- Bugs include map-load crash and "refresh" behavior.
- Official guide still exposes realm-center view adjustment as a user command.

### Audio

- 5.31 introduced a new sound system, better music, new options, and fan-observed CD player.
- Bugs include music stopping after a while.

### Screenshot / Capture

- Pre/post 5.31 screenshot behavior is important because justice evidence depended on client screenshots.
- Nexus Atlas records Scroll Lock capture requirements and Shift+Scroll Lock BMP advice.
- 5.31 reportedly saved screenshots as JPG.

### UI Lists And Panels

- Spell/equipment/item lists were affected by 5.31/5.32.
- Later 2010 ideas mention stretchable Group/Items/Spells tabs and group-member vita/mana bars.
- Current KRU guide remains keyboard/list heavy; current Korean Baram is menu/UI-heavy.

### Account, Billing, And Updater

- US commercial launch was pay-to-play.
- 2003 Singapore code-sales support used Nexon game codes across NexusTK, Dark Ages, and Shattered Galaxy.
- 2005 KRU transition changed account/support domains and branding.
- Current KRU official page has full installer plus updater repair packages.
