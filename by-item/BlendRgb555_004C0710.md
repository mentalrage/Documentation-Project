## Behavior

`BlendRgb555_4C0710` blends two RGB555 colors using a 0..32 weight. It is the single-pixel counterpart to the RGB555 span/blit helpers at [UID:0000YD][0x0045fa00-0x004604f4.Rgb555565BlitHelpers](by-memory/0x0045fa00-0x004604f4.Rgb555565BlitHelpers.md), and belongs to retained old-mode or compatibility drawing code.
