## File Role

`ScreenFadeOut.cpp` is the best current direct source-file owner for the `ScreenFadeOut` Pane-derived fade transition class. The class is a generic UI-core overlay that schedules fade timing, attaches over g_pScreenPane bounds on g_pStatusPaneLayer, and invalidates g_pBackPane or g_pScreenPane bounds during destruction. Pane supplies inherited EventHandler/TimerHandler facets; ScreenFadeOut adds a natural five-field tail.

This file should remain adjacent to, but separate from, [UID:0000NA][ScreenDimmer](by-file/ScreenDimmer.md). `ScreenDimmer.cpp` owns the modal dimmer singleton, dimmer factory, dim-level paint behavior, and `g_pScreenDimmer` lifecycle. `ScreenFadeOut.cpp` owns the separate fade class, its timer/fade fields, its vtable/RTTI family, and the compiler-generated adjustor thunks caused by the class layout.
