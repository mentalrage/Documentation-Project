### Supervisor-only five-role persistence handoff

This is the declarative `POWERSHELL_FILE_REPLACE_V1` acceptance contract, not an executable package. It uses only supported direct PowerShell/.NET primitives: `System.IO.FileStream` opened with `FileMode.CreateNew`, `FileAccess.Write`, `FileShare.None`, and `FileOptions.WriteThrough`, followed by `Flush(true)` and disposal, for each copied regular file; and exactly one direct `[System.IO.File]::Replace($source,$destination,$null,$true)` call for each replacement. On Windows this is the same-volume `ReplaceFileW` namespace primitive exposed by .NET. The null backup argument prevents an implicit side backup and `ignoreMetadataErrors:true` prevents metadata-copy errors from substituting a second operation. The source for each replacement is a disposable candidate, so verified TX and BACKUP remain source-preserving closed-route receipts. Directory-handle `FlushFileBuffers` is neither invoked nor required: the observed `Access is denied` result and the policy-blocked custom harness are recorded as `DIRECTORY_FLUSH_UNAVAILABLE`, and the supported replacement protocol uses file `Flush(true)` plus independent closed-process namespace persistence receipts instead.

Define `CANON` as the independently resolved canonical IDB, `BACKUP` as a byte-identical sealed baseline copy, and `TX` as a distinct transaction copy. Define `PROMOTION_CANDIDATE` and `RESTORE_CANDIDATE` as fresh collision-free names in the exact sealed CANON parent. A `CLOSED_FILE_RECEIPT` is collected only after all producing handles and processes are closed. It uses direct `Resolve-Path -LiteralPath`, `Get-Item -LiteralPath -Force`, `Get-FileHash -Algorithm SHA256`, `Get-Item -Stream *`, `fsutil file queryfileid`, `fsutil hardlink list`, and volume-info queries to record normalized absolute/resolved path, exact parent path and parent file identity, volume identity, regular-file attributes, reparse status, file identity, hardlink count and names, all streams, size, UTC write time, and SHA256, followed by a successful direct read-only `FileStream` `FileShare.None` open/close. It requires a regular non-reparse file, one hardlink at the expected path, only the unnamed data stream, and no alias. A `CLOSED_NAMESPACE_RECEIPT` comes from a fresh independent PowerShell process and uses `Get-ChildItem -LiteralPath -Force` to record the sealed parent's resolved identity/volume plus a case-insensitive exact-name enumeration for every protocol role, each required presence/absence, and a new `CLOSED_FILE_RECEIPT` for every present role. Two consecutive fresh-process receipts must agree before a namespace transition is accepted. They are observable post-close Windows file/namespace persistence proof; runtime attestation never substitutes for them. Once BACKUP has a valid baseline receipt, every later pre-promotion failure preserves both CANON and BACKUP byte-for-byte and by path/identity; only receipt-bound TX or candidate artifacts may be removed, and no restoration is attempted before a promotion transition.

1. **Pre-IDB capability gate.** Resolve CANON and its parent without opening an IDB. Require the complete parent chain and CANON to be non-reparse, and seal CANON's `CLOSED_FILE_RECEIPT`. In that exact parent, choose unique nonce-bearing `PROBE_SOURCE` and `PROBE_DESTINATION` names absent from a case-insensitive enumeration and distinct from every planned role. Create both with `FileMode.CreateNew`; write distinct nonce payloads with the direct write-through `FileStream`, call `Flush(true)`, dispose every handle, and take independent receipts. Invoke exactly once `[System.IO.File]::Replace($PROBE_SOURCE,$PROBE_DESTINATION,$null,$true)`, with no retry. Two fresh-process namespace receipts must prove source absent, destination present with the former source's exact size/SHA256 and a fresh regular-file identity, parent/volume unchanged, and CANON unchanged. After exact identity/hash authorization, delete only PROBE_DESTINATION with direct `[System.IO.File]::Delete`, then require two fresh-process namespace receipts proving both probe names absent and CANON unchanged. Known directory-handle access denial or harness policy block selects this protocol and does not fail the gate. A `File.Replace` exception, missing atomic consumption, collision, receipt disagreement, alias/reparse/hardlink/ADS defect, exclusive-open failure, CANON change, or probe cleanup residue stops before BACKUP/TX creation, `idb_open`, or any mutation; the supervisor records the exact exception/receipts and never falls back to rename/copy-overwrite or a custom harness.
2. **Gate-time role creation and receipts.** With the capability gate passed, create BACKUP and initial TX from the closed CANON through separate source read streams and separate destination write-through `FileStream` calls using `CreateNew`, `FileShare.None`, `Flush(true)`, and full disposal. Two consecutive fresh-process receipts must prove CANON, BACKUP, and TX are absolute, pairwise path-distinct and case-fold-distinct, pairwise file-identity-distinct regular files in the same exact parent and volume, with no hardlink, ADS, reparse, or final-path alias, and byte-identical size/SHA256. Require both candidate names absent. If BACKUP creation or verification itself fails, remove only the exact newly created invalid BACKUP after identity authorization, prove its absence, and stop with CANON unchanged before TX or any IDB route exists. After BACKUP seals, it is immutable: a TX collision/creation/receipt failure removes only exact receipt-bound TX, proves TX absence, and preserves sealed CANON/BACKUP unchanged. No failure in this step opens an IDB or permits mutation.
3. **Public surface and startup.** At startup and every boundary below, public `tools/list` must expose exactly one `runtime_attestation`, expose no `runtime/attest`, and prove current `idb_save` input `{database,path}` and result `{ok,error?,path}` plus the `idb_open`, `make_data`, `set_function_comments`, and `set_address_comments` schemas used above. Record and attestation-bind every active stateful worker route, including unrelated sessions, while proving path/session/PID/nonces do not overlap this transaction.
4. **Indivisible runtime package.** Each attestation must have top-level `schema_version:1`, `ok:true`, `errors:[]`; listener role/freshness/endpoint/port/PID/parent/generation/nonce and loaded-module rows sealed to the listener-specific baseline, with no listener-local `ok` or `errors` requirement; and target worker route/session/path, role `stateful_worker`, freshness/PID/parent/generation/nonce and loaded-module rows sealed to the worker-specific baseline plus worker-local `schema_version:1`, `ok:true`, `errors:[]`. Every role's module row requires exact `file`, `spec_origin`, `resolved_path`, fail-closed source-root containment, size, timestamps, and SHA256; `relative_path` is not expected from the response. Role manifests are checked only against their own baselines, never for cross-role equality. A missing, duplicated, stale, mismatched, unbound-active, or extra target route fails closed.
5. **DB mutation role.** Freshly call `idb_open({path:TX,run_auto_analysis:false})` on isolated TX and bind only its returned database identifier as `DB`; prove canonical/backup routes are absent. Recheck every literal prestate, collision, boundary, byte, xref, comment, frame, type, and neighbor above. Apply exactly 42 accepted mutations to the returned DB only. Immediately before and after every individual stateful mutation request, and again before save, repeat public-surface/runtime checks and full item-level readback: 19 exact 40-byte pools/names/types/comments, 4 exact compiler/EH comments, all six functions, three UDTs, cleanup, six padding items, 49 wrappers, bytes, bounds, xrefs, callees, and neighbors. Any error or extra mutation aborts with no save; after route retirement, unchanged CANON/BACKUP receipts and authorized TX disposal/no-residue proof close the failed transaction.
6. **Sole save and physical TX receipt.** After another complete boundary check, the only persistence call is literal `idb_save({database:DB,path:TX})`; require exact result `{ok:true,error absent-or-empty,path:exact normalized TX}`. Runtime attestation is not physical proof. Retire DB's route, close all TX handles, prove PID termination/non-reuse and no active session for TX, obtain a successful read-only exclusive Windows `FileShare.None` handle and close it, then obtain two agreeing fresh-process `CLOSED_FILE_RECEIPT`s for the physical TX. A stale inactive session record is acceptable only when no live route/process/handle exists.
7. **DB_VERIFY role.** Freshly call `idb_open({path:TX,run_auto_analysis:false})` and bind its distinct returned identifier as `DB_VERIFY`; this role is readback-only and never mutates or saves. Reattest and reread the entire intended/protected state listed in step 5; require exactly 42 changes and no other entity, byte, xref, type, frame, comment-channel, item-boundary, or neighbor delta. Close DB_VERIFY without save, retire its route, prove PID non-reuse/no live process or handle with `FileShare.None`, and independently confirm unchanged post-save TX size/SHA256/file identity with two agreeing fresh-process receipts.
8. **Pre-promotion sealed gate and PROMOTION_CANDIDATE.** With every IDA route closed, obtain one fresh `CLOSED_NAMESPACE_RECEIPT` and exact per-file receipts before creating the candidate. Require CANON and BACKUP still equal the original baseline, TX still equals DB_VERIFY's saved receipt, all three remain pairwise non-aliasing in the sealed parent/volume, both candidate names are absent without case-fold collisions, and all handles can be exclusively reopened. Create PROMOTION_CANDIDATE from verified TX with the exact write-through `CreateNew`/`Flush(true)`/dispose primitive. Two independent fresh-process receipts must prove its distinct regular-file identity, same exact parent/volume, no hardlink/ADS/reparse/final-path alias, exact TX size/SHA256, and stable closed state, while proving CANON/BACKUP/TX unchanged. Any pre-promotion failure authorizes deletion only of the exact receipt-bound unconsumed candidate; two fresh-process namespace receipts must prove no candidate residue and unchanged CANON/BACKUP. No restore or alternate promotion occurs.
9. **One-attempt promotion and DB_FINAL role.** From a new PowerShell process with every database and file handle closed, invoke exactly once `[System.IO.File]::Replace($PROMOTION_CANDIDATE,$CANON,$null,$true)`; do not retry even if the call's result is ambiguous. TX and BACKUP are not source arguments and remain unchanged. Two new-process namespace receipts must prove PROMOTION_CANDIDATE absent, CANON present as a regular non-reparse single-link unnamed-stream file with exact TX size/SHA256, sealed parent/volume unchanged, BACKUP still exact original baseline, TX still exact DB_VERIFY receipt, RESTORE_CANDIDATE absent, and no case-folded residue. Freshly call `idb_open({path:CANON,run_auto_analysis:false})`, bind its distinct returned identifier as read-only `DB_FINAL`, and run the complete runtime/entity acceptance proof. Close without save, retire its route, prove PID non-reuse and `FileShare.None`, and obtain two final fresh-process namespace/file receipts proving canonical TX content and physical identity stability plus unchanged BACKUP/TX and no candidate residue.
10. **Post-promotion ambiguity and DB_ROLLBACK classification.** A thrown replacement exception, missing/ambiguous post-call receipt, failed DB_FINAL, or failed canonical acceptance is post-promotion state and never re-enters the pre-promotion cleanup path or retries replacement. Retire the failed canonical route and close every handle first. A fresh `idb_open({path:CANON,run_auto_analysis:false})` must return a new read-only `DB_ROLLBACK` identifier for the failed/ambiguous CANON itself, never BACKUP, TX, a candidate, or any earlier role. DB_ROLLBACK records the complete public/runtime/entity state of failed CANON; independent physical receipts record its exact namespace/file state. Close without save, retire the route, prove PID non-reuse and `FileShare.None`, and preserve this classification before restoration.
11. **BACKUP-only restoration and DB_RESTORE role.** Only after DB_ROLLBACK classification may closed-route BACKUP become the restoration source. Revalidate BACKUP against the original baseline and require RESTORE_CANDIDATE absent without collision. Create RESTORE_CANDIDATE by copying BACKUP through the same write-through `CreateNew`/`Flush(true)`/dispose primitive; two fresh-process receipts must prove exact BACKUP size/SHA256, a distinct regular-file identity, same sealed parent/volume, and no alias/hardlink/ADS/reparse while BACKUP remains unchanged. From a new process invoke exactly once `[System.IO.File]::Replace($RESTORE_CANDIDATE,$CANON,$null,$true)` with no retry. Two fresh-process namespace receipts must prove the candidate consumed with no residue, CANON exactly equals the original BACKUP baseline, BACKUP remains unchanged/source-preserved, and TX remains the classified saved transaction. Freshly call `idb_open({path:CANON,run_auto_analysis:false})`, bind its distinct returned identifier as read-only `DB_RESTORE`, and verify the complete original runtime/entity baseline. Close without save, retire its route, prove PID non-reuse and `FileShare.None`, and obtain two final receipts proving stable original CANON content and no candidate residue. Any restoration ambiguity stops with DB_ROLLBACK and physical receipts preserved; it never retries, improvises another source, or reuses DB, DB_VERIFY, DB_FINAL, DB_ROLLBACK, or DB_RESTORE.
