### Arithmetic, signedness, and overflow/truncation

- `movzx edx, word ptr [ecx+0FAh]` reads the old stored low word.
- The stack argument is loaded into `eax`, added to the old low word, and then explicitly normalized with `movzx eax, ax`.
- The machine therefore computes the result modulo `2^16`, compares old and new low words, and stores only the low word.
- Current source `short value = static_cast<short>(m_rangeCurrent + delta);` is behavior-exact for target MSVC:

  - target MSVC conversion back to `short` truncates to the low 16 bits, matching `movzx eax,ax`;
  - equality between the two signed shorts is equivalent to the observed word comparison;
  - negative deltas and wraparound are intentionally allowed.
